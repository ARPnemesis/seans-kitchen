## This Week's Dishes

### 1. Chipotle Chicken Tinga Rice Bowls
*Mexican · weeknight · 35 min · ~45g protein · ~580 cal*

Chicken thighs seared, then braised down in a smoky sauce of fire-roasted tomato, chipotle in adobo, and cumin, shredded back into the pan until the sauce clings. Served over cilantro-lime rice with black beans, avocado, and cotija. Mexican and Latin dishes are a 5★ average across four dishes for you — carne asada, salmon tacos, and the Southwest sweet potatoes all landed. This is the reheat-friendly member of that family: shredded meat in sauce is genuinely better on day two.

### 2. Thai Red Curry Ground Turkey with Green Beans & Jasmine Rice
*Thai · weeknight · 30 min · ~43g protein · ~565 cal*

Thai Basil Chicken Bowls were "amazing!! loved these!" — a 5★ and the only Thai data point on file, five weeks back. This is the fast version: ground turkey browned hard, red curry paste bloomed in the fat, coconut milk, fish sauce, brown sugar, green beans and red pepper simmered in. **This is deliberately the anti-Cajun-pasta** — coconut milk is exactly the sauce body The Critic prescribed to replace yogurt, and it reheats without breaking. The quickest dish of the week.

### 3. Smothered Pork Tenderloin Medallions with Mushroom Gravy & Smashed Potatoes
*American comfort · weeknight · 40 min · ~47g protein · ~575 cal*

American comfort is your broadest and most consistent winner — five dishes, 5★ average. This is that category at its most leftover-friendly: pork tenderloin medallions seared to a proper crust, then finished in a mushroom-and-onion pan gravy over rustic smashed potatoes. **Read the cut carefully — this is tenderloin, not a chop.** You told the kitchen you're "not a huge pork chop guy" and that rule stands; this is a different cut entirely, and it sits in gravy rather than drying out on a plate. Comes with a thermometer target (140°F off the heat, 145°F rested) so the doneness isn't a guess.

### 4. Moroccan Ground Lamb & Chickpea Skillet with Couscous
*Moroccan / North African · weeknight · 30 min · ~41g protein · ~590 cal*

Middle Eastern is a 4.5★ average for you — shawarma at 5★, harissa chicken at 4★ — and this pushes further west into North Africa. Ground lamb browned hard, then cumin, coriander, cinnamon and smoked paprika bloomed in the fat, simmered with crushed tomato and chickpeas into a thick skillet stew over lemon couscous, finished with toasted almonds and mint. **Ground lamb is a new protein for this kitchen** — the recipe card carries a straight ground-beef swap if it reads too strong. Warmed bread on the side, per your own discovery.

### 5. Hungarian Beef Goulash with Buttered Egg Noodles
*Hungarian · **weekend** · 2 hr 15 min, 25 active · ~46g protein · ~595 cal*

The weekend dish, and the one with the biggest payoff. Beef chuck seared hard, then braised low and slow with three tablespoons of sweet Hungarian paprika, caraway, onions, peppers and carrots until it shreds under a spoon. Buttered egg noodles, a spoonful of sour cream at the bowl, and **warmed baguette slices stood around the rim** — your own invention from the Italian sausage skillet, which you called a "perfect use case," now built into a dish on purpose. Mostly hands-off after the first 25 minutes. Makes more than two servings; goulash is at its best on day three.
