## This Week's Dishes

### 1. Cuban Picadillo with Black Beans & Rice
*Cuban · Weeknight · 35 min · ~42g protein · ~575 cal*

Ground beef simmered down with tomato, cumin and oregano, then finished with the sweet-salty-briny triangle that defines the dish — raisins, green olives, capers. Served over rice with black beans, lime and cilantro. This is a saucy skillet that genuinely improves overnight, and it's the week's most reliable microwave reheat. It's also a deliberate second look at Cuban cuisine in a format that isn't a pork chop.

**Reheat: microwave, covered, 2 min.**

### 2. Korean Braised Chicken & Potatoes (Dak-Dori-Tang)
*Korean · Weeknight · 45 min · ~44g protein · ~570 cal*

Chicken thighs braised with potatoes, carrot and onion in a gochujang-and-gochugaru sauce until the sauce reduces to a glaze that clings. Korean is a 4.5★ cuisine here and this is a completely different animal from the bulgogi bowls — slow, deep and spicy rather than quick and sweet. Bone-in thighs, cooked to 175°F where the dark meat actually wants to be. A braise, so day two is better than day one.

**Reheat: microwave, covered, 2–3 min.**

### 3. Baked Rigatoni with Italian Sausage & Ricotta
*Italian · Weeknight · 50 min (20 active, 25 hands-off) · ~46g protein · ~595 cal*

The oven-reheat play, built directly on the stuffed-pepper lesson. Rigatoni boiled short, tossed with browned Italian sausage in a tomato sauce, dolloped — not stirred — with ricotta, blanketed in mozzarella and parmesan, and baked until the edges brown. The highest-protein dish of the week. Honest timing: this is fifty minutes, but thirty of them are the oven's.

**Reheat: 350°F oven, covered with foil, 15–18 min. This is the best-reheating dish on the board.**

### 4. Mississippi Pot Roast
*American Comfort · **Weekend** · 8 hrs slow cooker (15 min prep) · ~40g protein · ~480 cal*

Back by popular demand and the Critic's recommendation. Chuck roast, au jus packet, ranch packet, pepperoncini, a stick of butter, eight hours, no lid-lifting. Fifteen minutes of your attention for roughly four meals. Nothing has been changed except the recipe card, which now names the oven reheat and suggests the leftover sandwich.

**Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 15 min, with the juices spooned over.**

### 5. Cajun Honey-Butter Shrimp Bowls
*Cajun/Creole · **Weekend — best eaten fresh** · 25 min · ~40g protein · ~525 cal*

Six weeks in the waiting room, finally cleared. Cornstarch-dredged shrimp seared hard, glazed in honey-butter with garlic and lemon, over jasmine rice with seasoned bell pepper and green onion. Quick to cook — it's on the weekend for freshness, not for effort. Eat it the night you make it.

**Reheat: skillet, medium-low, 60–90 sec — or better, don't.**
