## This Week's Dishes

1. **Jamaican Jerk Chicken Thighs with Coconut Rice & Black Beans** — allspice-and-thyme jerk rub charred onto boneless thighs, served over coconut-simmered jasmine rice folded with black beans. Caribbean is new to the rotation, and this is its best entry point.

2. **Philly Cheesesteak Stuffed Peppers** — ground beef, mushrooms, and onion in a Worcestershire pan sauce, packed into roasted bell peppers and blistered under provolone. American comfort is your 5★ cuisine; this is the fastest, cleanest expression of it.

3. **Bang Bang Salmon Rice Bowls** — cornstarch-crusted salmon cubes seared hard, over rice with cucumber and avocado, under a sweet-chili-sriracha-mayo drizzle. Salmon is running 4.7★ across three prep styles now; this is a fourth, and the trendiest dinner on the internet this month.

4. **Vietnamese Lemongrass Pork Meatball Bowls** — caramelized lemongrass pork over rice vermicelli with cucumber, carrot, mint, and cilantro, dressed at the table with nuoc cham. Bright, herb-forward, and the antidote to a heavy week.

5. **Spanish Shrimp & Chorizo Paella** — the weekend build. Saffron stock, a slow sofrito, cured chorizo, and shrimp, finished with a proper socarrat crust on the bottom of the pan. Forty-five unhurried minutes and one wide skillet.
