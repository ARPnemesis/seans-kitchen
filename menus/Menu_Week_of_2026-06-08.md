## 🌯 Chicken Shawarma Bowl
**Weeknight | 25 min | ~45g protein | ~520 cal per serving**

### Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1.25 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, pinch chili flakes
- 1 cup dry white rice
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 lemon (juice + zest)
- Salt, pepper, olive oil
- 4 pita breads (optional)

### Method
1. Combine cumin, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, chili flakes, salt, and pepper. Coat chicken thighs evenly.
2. Cook rice according to package directions.
3. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook chicken 5–6 min per side until cooked through. Rest 3 min, slice.
4. Mix yogurt with lemon zest, a squeeze of lemon, and salt for the sauce.
5. Build bowls: rice base, sliced chicken, diced cucumber, a generous dollop of yogurt sauce, extra lemon squeeze. Serve with warmed pita if desired.

## 🌶️ Gochujang Ground Turkey Bowl
**Weeknight | 20 min | ~38g protein | ~480 cal per serving**

### Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced thin
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 1 cup dry white rice
- Soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic powder to taste

### Method
1. Cook rice.
2. Brown ground turkey in a hot skillet over medium-high, breaking it up, 5–6 min. Season with garlic powder and a splash of soy sauce.
3. Add bell pepper and broccoli. Stir-fry 3–4 min until just tender.
4. Mix gochujang, oyster sauce, honey, and a drizzle of sesame oil. Add to the pan, toss everything to coat, cook 1 min.
5. Serve over rice, top with scallions.

## 🍗 Garlic Butter Chicken Thighs & Broccoli
**Weeknight | 25 min | ~42g protein | ~440 cal per serving**

### Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1.25 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs (from same pack as shawarma dish)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 large head broccoli, cut into florets
- Salt, pepper, chili flakes, olive oil

### Method
1. Pat chicken dry, season generously with salt, pepper, and chili flakes.
2. Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken thighs 4–5 min per side until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
3. In the same pan, melt butter over medium heat. Add minced garlic, sauté 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add broccoli, toss in garlic butter, sauté 4–5 min until bright green and tender-crisp. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Return chicken to the pan, spoon garlic butter over everything. Serve immediately.

## 🐟 Honey Garlic Salmon & Sesame Cucumber Salad
**Weekend | 25 min | ~38g protein | ~450 cal per serving**

### Ingredients (2 servings)
- 2 salmon fillets (6 oz each), skin on
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cucumber, sliced thin
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- Salt, pepper, olive oil

### Method
1. Mix honey, soy sauce, and minced garlic for the glaze.
2. Season salmon fillets with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high.
3. Place salmon skin-side down, press gently, cook 3–4 min. Flip, pour glaze over fillets, cook another 3–4 min basting frequently. Remove when caramelized and cooked through.
4. Toss cucumber and scallions with rice vinegar, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and a pinch of salt.
5. Plate cucumber salad alongside glazed salmon. Serve with rice or on its own.

## 🍖 Mississippi Pot Roast
**Weekend | 8 hrs slow cooker (15 min prep) | ~40g protein | ~480 cal per serving**

### Ingredients (2–3 servings)
- 2 lbs chuck roast
- 1 packet au jus gravy mix
- 1 packet ranch dressing mix
- 8–10 pepperoncini peppers + splash of brine
- 4 tbsp butter
- Salt and pepper

### Method
1. Season chuck roast generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
2. Place in the slow cooker. Sprinkle au jus and ranch packets evenly over the top.
3. Add pepperoncini peppers around the roast with a splash of brine.
4. Lay butter on top in pats.
5. Cover and cook on LOW for 8 hours — don't open the lid mid-cook.
6. Shred with two forks directly in the slow cooker and mix into the juices.
7. Serve over mashed potatoes or with crusty bread.
