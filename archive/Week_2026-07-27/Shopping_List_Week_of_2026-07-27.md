## Shared-Ingredient Notes

- **Limes (4)** — the jerk rub, the bang bang sauce, and the nuoc cham all want fresh lime juice; 4 covers the juice plus wedges for two dishes.
- **Cilantro (2 bunches)** — jerk chicken, bang bang bowls, and meatball bowls. One bunch will not stretch.
- **Green onions (6 stalks / 2 bunches)** — jerk rice, bang bang bowls, meatball bowls, 2 each.
- **Two different rices** — jasmine for the jerk chicken and bang bang bowls, bomba/arborio for the paella. They are not interchangeable.
- **Yellow onions** — Philly and paella need one each; bought as a 3-lb bag, you'll have spares.
- **Shrimp** — recipe calls for ¾ lb but bags come in 1 lb; buy the pound and use it all.
