## The Dishes

*Full recipes in `Recipes/`.*

### 1. Vietnamese Lemongrass Pork Meatball Bowls
*Vietnamese · weeknight · 35 min · ~42 g protein · ~560 cal · **2 meals***

Caramelized lemongrass-and-fish-sauce pork meatballs over rice vermicelli, with quick-pickled carrot, cucumber, lettuce and a serious pile of mint and cilantro, all dressed at the table in nuoc cham. **The clean re-run** — cooked as written, with real lemongrass, for the first time. Ground pork is the most perishable thing on the board, so this wants the front of the week. Reheat: meatballs and noodles only, covered, 90 seconds — keep the salad cold and dress on day two.

### 2. Cajun Dirty Rice Skillet with Ground Beef & Andouille
*Cajun / Creole · weeknight · 40 min · ~44 g protein · ~590 cal · **3 meals***

One pot. Browned beef and crisped andouille, the trinity, and rice cooked in seasoned stock until every grain has taken on the spice. **Zero dairy** — which is precisely what the 1★ Cajun pasta had too much of. This is the highest-yield weeknight dish of the week and the one most likely to be better as a Wednesday lunch than a Tuesday dinner. The bell pepper is seasoned as it enters the pan, per your standing request.

### 3. Jamaican Jerk Chicken Thighs with Coconut Rice & Black Beans
*Caribbean · weeknight · 40 min · ~48 g protein · ~585 cal · **2 meals***

Allspice, thyme, scotch bonnet and brown sugar rubbed into chicken thighs and seared until the sugar chars, over coconut rice folded with black beans and green onion. **The one recycle on the board**, back exactly on schedule and by the Critic's explicit recommendation. Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 15 minutes — keep two lime wedges back for night two.

### 4. Chicken Karahi with Basmati & Naan
*Indian / Pakistani · weeknight · 40 min · ~50 g protein · ~575 cal · **2 meals***

Chicken breast in a masala of tomato, ginger, garlic and green chili cooked down hard in ghee until the fat separates — bold, ginger-forward, and **completely free of yogurt and cream**, which is the one thing butter chicken couldn't survive on a reheat. The highest-protein dish of the week. If this lands Thursday, freeze the chicken on arrival and thaw the night before.

### 5. Turkey Shepherd's Pie with Cheddar-Chive Mash
*British comfort · weekend · 55 min · ~45 g protein · ~580 cal · **3 meals***

Ground turkey in a thyme-and-Worcestershire gravy with carrot and peas, roofed in cheddar-chive mashed potato and baked until the ridges go golden. Oven-baked casseroles are the best-reheating format this kitchen has on record, and **you asked for mashed potatoes back on the table** — here they are, load-bearing. 🧊 **Freeze the turkey on arrival**; it's the most perishable item on the list and it sits at the back of the week. Aim for Sunday — Saturday is Ian's birthday.
