## ⚠️ NEW signal for the Chef — yogurt fatigue
Sean's Greek Meatball note is the key takeaway: *"the greek yogurt in every recipe is starting to get old."* Yogurt-forward sauces (tzatziki, yogurt marinades/cremas) are appearing too often. **Cap Greek/plain-yogurt-forward sauces at ~1 per week and vary sauce bases.** The yogurt-for-cream swap is still a win when used sparingly (butter chicken, shawarma) — the problem is frequency, not yogurt itself.

## Recommendations for the Chef
- Keep leaning on the proven winners: Korean (bulgogi), Latin (salmon tacos, carne asada), American comfort, salmon in varied formats.
- Honor the yogurt cap above — don't stack two yogurt-sauced dishes in one week.
- Continue the format rule: judge sauces and prep formats, not just proteins. No whole pork chops; no stir-fry-with-fries repeats.
- Reminder: dishes marked `(DROPPED … — not cooked)` never hit the table, so the no-repeat window does NOT apply to them — they're eligible for early reuse if they still fit preferences. (None new this week.)
