## 3. Recommendations for the Chef

1. **Score dishes for reheat.** Sean says it directly: two-portion meals, one adult, leftovers are next-day lunch — "almost my entire diet." Favor braises, skillets, saucy bean/grain dishes, stews, roasts, ground-meat dishes. Delicate quick-sear proteins (scallops, thin fish, shrimp) belong on a weekend or not at all, and the recipe card should say "best eaten fresh."
2. **Yogurt: hard rule now, not advisory.** The ~1/week cap stays, and on top of it — **never use yogurt as the body of a creamy pasta sauce or under a spice-forward seasoning.** Heavy cream, coconut milk, tomato, or a butter emulsion instead. Yogurt is fine as a finishing dollop or marinade.
3. **Whole-steak recipes need a doneness method.** High heat, thermometer target ~130–135°F for medium-rare, explicit rest time. The chimichurri dish was a 5★ that Sean had to fight for.
4. **Stop calling multi-component wraps/bowls ≤30 minutes.** The turkey lettuce wraps took ~40 minutes in Sean's kitchen. Time estimates on assembly-heavy dishes should run long, not short.
5. **Make produce counts loud on the shopping list.** Third shortfall in seven weeks. Write "LEMONS ×3 — need all three" and flag any item whose absence breaks the dish.
6. **Adopt two of Sean's own mods:** warmed baguette slices stood around the bowl rim as a sauce-sopper (his idea, "perfect use case" — build crusty bread into future saucy skillets); and for the ginger-sesame wraps, thicken the sauce and offer spinach tortillas as the wrap.
7. **Dropped-never-cooked dishes are exempt from the no-repeat window.** A dish annotated `(DROPPED … — not cooked)` never hit the table, so it did not consume a rotation slot and is eligible for early reuse if it still fits preferences. Nothing was dropped from week 07-20 — the only dish in this pool remains Cajun Honey-Butter Shrimp Bowls (dropped 06-29).
