## 1. Honey-Garlic Salmon with Asparagus & Broccoli

*Crispy salmon in a spicy-sweet honey-garlic glaze. One pan, weeknight-fast.*

**Serves 2 · ~38g protein · lower fat**

**Ingredients**
- 2 salmon fillets (~6 oz each)
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 1 head broccoli, cut into florets
- 3 tbsp honey
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for GF)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss asparagus and broccoli with olive oil, salt, pepper on a sheet pan; roast 10 min.
2. Whisk honey, garlic, soy, vinegar, chili flakes.
3. Push veg aside, add salmon skin-side down, brush generously with glaze. Roast 10–12 min until salmon flakes.
4. Spoon remaining glaze over the top before serving.

## 2. Skirt Steak Chimichurri Bowl

*Thin-sliced medium-rare skirt steak over rice with bright chimichurri and quick-pickled onions.*

**Serves 2 · ~42g protein**

**Ingredients**
- 12 oz skirt steak
- 1 cup rice (white or brown), cooked
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 1 cup parsley, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar (for chimichurri)
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes, salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Quick-pickle: cover sliced onion with red wine vinegar + pinch salt; set aside 15 min.
2. Chimichurri: mix parsley, garlic, olive oil, 1 tbsp vinegar, chili flakes, salt.
3. Season steak well, sear in a hot pan 2–3 min per side for medium-rare. Rest 5 min, slice against the grain.
4. Build bowls: rice, steak, chimichurri, pickled onions.

## 3. Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow Gai)

*The Bangkok street-food classic — ground chicken stir-fried with garlic, chili, and a mountain of Thai basil.*

**Serves 2 · ~40g protein**

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb ground chicken
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2–3 Thai chilies (or 1 tsp chili flakes), minced
- 1 cup Thai basil leaves
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp oil
- Steamed jasmine rice, 2 eggs (optional fried, to top)

**Method**
1. Heat oil, fry garlic and chili 30 sec.
2. Add ground chicken, break up, cook until no longer pink.
3. Add soy, oyster, fish sauce, sugar; toss 1 min.
4. Kill heat, fold in basil until just wilted. Serve over rice, topped with a fried egg.

## 4. Korean Beef Bibimbap Bowls

*Savory sesame-soy ground beef over rice with veg and a runny egg. Comfort and macros in one bowl.*

**Serves 2 · ~41g protein**

**Ingredients**
- 12 oz lean ground beef
- 1 cup rice, cooked
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 cup bean sprouts (or shredded cabbage)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 eggs, gochujang to taste, sesame seeds

**Method**
1. Brown beef; add soy, sesame oil, sugar, garlic. Simmer until glazed.
2. Quickly sauté spinach, carrot, sprouts separately with a pinch of salt.
3. Fry eggs sunny-side up.
4. Build bowls: rice, beef, veg arranged in sections, egg on top, gochujang and sesame seeds.

## 5. Garlic Shrimp & Cauliflower Rice Stir-Fry

*Lean, fast, and low-fat — plump garlic shrimp over cauliflower rice. The lightest night of the week.*

**Serves 2 · ~36g protein · low carb / low fat**

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 bag (12 oz) cauliflower rice
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 cups snap peas
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp olive oil, chili flakes, green onion

**Method**
1. Sauté shrimp with garlic in olive oil, 2 min per side until pink; remove.
2. In same pan, stir-fry bell pepper and snap peas 3 min.
3. Add cauliflower rice, soy, vinegar, sesame oil; cook 4 min.
4. Return shrimp, toss. Top with green onion and chili flakes.

## 6. Greek Chicken Souvlaki Bowls with Tzatziki

*Lemon-oregano marinated chicken, cool tzatziki, crisp salad. Mediterranean and protein-packed.*

**Serves 2 · ~44g protein**

**Ingredients**
- 1.25 lb chicken breast, cubed
- 3 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tsp dried oregano, 3 cloves garlic
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 cup cooked rice or 1 can chickpeas
- Cherry tomatoes, red onion, feta (optional)

**Method**
1. Marinate chicken in olive oil, lemon, oregano, half the garlic, salt — 30 min (or overnight).
2. Tzatziki: yogurt + cucumber + remaining garlic + squeeze of lemon + salt.
3. Sear or grill chicken until cooked through, ~6–8 min.
4. Build bowls: rice or chickpeas, chicken, tomatoes, onion, feta, big dollop of tzatziki.

## 7. Sweet Potato Ground Beef Bowl with Cottage Cheese & Hot Honey

*This week's viral plate — and it lives up to the hype. Roasted sweet potato, seasoned beef, cottage cheese, avocado, hot honey drizzle.*

**Serves 2 · ~45g protein**

**Ingredients**
- 12 oz lean ground beef
- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 tbsp honey + 1/2 tsp chili flakes (= hot honey)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cumin, garlic powder
- 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oven 425°F. Toss sweet potato in olive oil + salt; roast 25 min.
2. Brown beef with paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt.
3. Warm honey with chili flakes for 30 sec to make hot honey.
4. Build bowls: sweet potato, beef, scoop of cottage cheese, avocado, drizzle of hot honey.
