## b) Running patterns across nine weeks

**37 dish ratings; 32 at 4★+ (86%). Two consecutive weeks with nothing below 4★, no "Cook again: No," and no difficulty miss.**

1. **FORMAT BEATS PROTEIN — sixth confirmation, and this week's is the cleanest ever.** Whole pork **chops**: 3★ / Cook again: No. Pork **tenderloin medallions in gravy**: 5★ / "wouldn't change anything." Same animal, opposite verdicts. Prior five: ground pork fine while chops missed; Lomo Saltado missed while every other beef format hit 5★; Greek meatballs missed on the tzatziki format while chicken was otherwise 5★; Cajun shrimp missed on a yogurt sauce; shrimp returned 4★ the instant the sauce changed. **Judge the format, the sauce and the technique. Never the ingredient.**
2. **Pork's verdict has flipped.** Pork is now **4.3★ across 4 dishes**. Tenderloin and ground pork are both on the roster. Only the chop format stays off.
3. **Sourcing has overtaken cooking as the kitchen's biggest source of lost stars — and it now has two failure modes.** *Cart-building gaps* (lemongrass, chorizo, honey, lemons) are fixable by writing a better list. *The store itself* — no lamb on the shelf, rancid stew meat — is not. Four consecutive weeks have lost a defining ingredient.
4. **Reheat quality keeps paying.** "Reheated well" appeared unprompted, second week running, on a card that named its reheat method. The criterion is being read and followed.
5. **Difficulty estimates are calibrated.** 3-for-3 "as expected" after 07-27's 5-for-5.
6. **Relative scoring is real.** A single 4★ in a strong week is not a demotion.

## c) Recommendations for the Chef — week of 2026-08-17

1. 🔥 **The hoagie is due.** The Philly Cheesesteak Stuffed Peppers clear their no-repeat window this week, and Sean asked for **the cheesesteak filling on a hoagie** by name. It is the oldest open standing request on the books, it is a 5★ dish, and it drew the best reheat note the kitchen has on file. Serve it.
2. 🥩 **Never put a highly perishable raw protein in the back half of the week again** unless the card says **"FREEZE ON ARRIVAL — thaw in the fridge the night before."** Groceries land once, at the top of the week; a Thursday–Sunday dish sits four to six days. This rule cost the kitchen an entire dinner on 08-09. Late slots belong to roasts, cured items and pantry-forward dishes.
3. 🧂 **Name a sanctioned fallback for any specialty ingredient.** "Ground lamb — if unavailable, 85/15 ground beef; the dish still works." Sean will substitute regardless; telling him what to substitute costs nothing and protects the score.
4. 🥬 **Vegetable ratio: ~6–8 oz ceiling for a single supporting vegetable in a 2-serving dish.** 10 oz of green beans cost the Thai Red Curry a star. Protein and sauce lead; the vegetable supports. Put it on the card.
5. 🌍 **Expand into Moroccan / North African.** A 5★ achieved with the *wrong protein* is a strong read on the cuisine, not the recipe. Also worth a clean re-run of the Moroccan skillet with actual lamb if the store has it — but it is already a 5★ as cooked.
6. 🍖 **Pan-gravy and medallion formats are proven.** Smothered tenderloin was the week's standout. This format is weeknight-friendly, reheats in a covered pan, and is cheap. Build on it.
7. **Dropped-not-cooked dishes never hit the table, so the no-repeat window does NOT apply to them.** They are eligible for early reuse if they still fit preferences. Current pool: **Hungarian Beef Goulash** (08-09, meat spoiled — Sean explicitly still wants it), **Korean Braised Chicken & Potatoes / Dak-Dori-Tang** (08-09, dropped for slate size — never a negative signal), and **Chipotle Chicken Tinga Rice Bowls** if it did not cook in week 08-10.
8. **Slate size is a budget of meals, not a count of dishes.** If a dish carries into 08-17, or if the menu contains a 4+ portion roast or braise, build **four** other dishes, not five.
9. **Zero Greek yogurt as the body of a creamy or spice-forward sauce.** Holding at zero for three weeks running — keep it there.
10. **Saturday 08-15 was deliberately left empty for a fishing trip.** If Saturdays keep going empty through the summer, stop placing the weekend dish there.
