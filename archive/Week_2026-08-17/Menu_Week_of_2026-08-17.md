### 1. Philly Cheesesteak Hoagies
*American comfort · weeknight · 30 min · ~48g protein · ~625 cal*

Shaved steak seared hard in a screaming pan, seasoned onions and peppers folded through, six slices of provolone melted directly into the meat, and the whole pile slid into a buttered, toasted hoagie roll. **This is the standing request you made on 07-27, finally delivered** — and the peppers are salted before the pan, per your second request from the same note. Reheat: 350°F oven, 12–15 min in foil; store the filling and the roll separately for lunch.

### 2. Harissa-Honey Salmon with Lemon-Herb Rice & Blistered Green Beans
*North African / Moroccan · weeknight · 30 min · ~44g protein · ~575 cal*

Salmon dusted in cornstarch and seared to a crust — your own note on the Bang Bang salmon, applied again — then glazed with harissa, honey, lemon and cumin and finished in a hot oven. Lemon-herb rice underneath, **8 oz of blistered green beans alongside and not an ounce more.** Pulled at 130°F, because salmon that hits 145°F in the pan is dry on the plate and worse in the lunchbox. Reheat: 300°F oven, covered, 8–10 min — never full-power microwave.

📖 [Recipe doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yARx85tOP0lJi3u5WQflX-yrLcXLNkdUphjHFHUONYY/edit)

### 3. Smoky Chipotle Pork & Black Bean Chili
*Tex-Mex / American comfort · weeknight · 40 min · ~46g protein · ~545 cal*

Ground pork browned in large pieces for real crust, chipotles in adobo and the spices bloomed in the fat for a full minute, then black beans, fire-roasted tomatoes and stock simmered down to something spoonable. **Nine weeks and the kitchen has never once made a chili** — and American comfort is your broadest winner at 5★ across six dishes. Makes more than two servings on purpose. Reheat: microwave, covered, 2–3 min; toppings fresh each time.

📖 [Recipe doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1VjJyTzTaAE-Nj0oBZGqxXsyLWd5kmmxEcG7_XaRxxhM/edit)

### 4. Filipino Chicken Adobo with Garlic Rice
*Filipino · weeknight · 40 min (10 min active) · ~47g protein · ~580 cal*

**Brand-new cuisine, and the lowest-risk dish on the board.** Chicken thighs braised in soy sauce, vinegar, eight cloves of garlic, bay and peppercorns, then reduced hard into a dark, glossy, salty-sour glaze. Served over garlic fried rice. **Almost every ingredient is already in your pantry** — there is nothing here for King Soopers to be out of. Reheat: microwave, covered, 2–3 min — **this is the best reheat on the board**; adobo is a preserved dish by design and genuinely improves for three days.

📖 [Recipe doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Fo22aWf6G3fvxFyvLT_VatS2C4JvZzN0MSZ2M1g2qF8/edit)

### 5. Mongolian Beef with Jasmine Rice & Charred Scallions
*Chinese · **weekend** · 30 min · ~45g protein · ~590 cal*

🧊 **FREEZE THE STEAK ON ARRIVAL — and this one you'll actually want to.** Flank steak slices far cleaner semi-frozen, so the freeze is the technique, not a compromise. Cornstarch-coated strips seared hard in two batches, a soy-brown-sugar-ginger sauce reduced to a gloss, broccoli and charred scallion batons tossed through. **Second brand-new cuisine this week**, sitting right beside your 5★ Korean bulgogi. Reheat: microwave, covered, 2 min — the sauce coating is why this survives the fridge when a dry stir-fry wouldn't.
