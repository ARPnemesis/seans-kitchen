## 🚨 Do-Not-Skip Ingredients — these ARE the dishes

- **HARISSA PASTE** (jar or tube, ~6 oz) — *this is the salmon. Without it the dish is just baked fish.* International aisle or specialty condiments. **If King Soopers doesn't carry it, the recipe card has a sanctioned fallback** (tomato paste + smoked paprika + cayenne + cumin).
- **CHIPOTLE PEPPERS IN ADOBO** (7 oz can) — the smoke in the chili. Nothing else does this job.
- **HOAGIE ROLLS** and **PROVOLONE** — the whole point of this week's cheesesteak is that it's on a roll. Do not come home without them.
- **FLANK STEAK** — must be a fast-sear cut. Skirt or sirloin are fine fallbacks; **stew meat and chuck are not** and will ruin it.
- **SOY SAUCE — you need a full cup this week** (½ cup adobo + ½ cup Mongolian beef). This is the one pantry item most likely to run out mid-week.
