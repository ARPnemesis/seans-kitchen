## 🚨 Read This First — Identity Ingredients

These four carry their dishes. If the store is out, **change the plan, don't substitute** — two dishes lost a star last week to exactly this.

- **GOCHUGARU (Korean coarse chili flakes)** — *this IS the Korean braise.* Cayenne and crushed red pepper are not the same thing; they're hot without the fruitiness. Korean/international aisle, usually a red bag.
- **GOCHUJANG (Korean chili paste)** — the other half of that sauce. Red tub.
- **CAJUN SEASONING** — the shrimp bowls are the Cajun blend plus honey-butter. Without it you have buttery shrimp.
- **CHUCK ROAST** — the only cut that works for the pot roast. Not round, not brisket, not "roast beef." Eight hours is what renders chuck's fat, and a leaner cut comes out dry and stringy.
