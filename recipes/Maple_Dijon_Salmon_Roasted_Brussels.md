# Maple-Dijon Glazed Salmon with Roasted Brussels Sprouts
*American · Weeknight · 30 min · ~42g protein · ~500 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 2 salmon fillets (~6 oz each)
- 1 lb Brussels sprouts, halved
- 2 tbsp **maple syrup**
- 1 tbsp **Dijon mustard**
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for sprouts) + 1 tsp (for glaze)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp smoked paprika

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Toss halved Brussels sprouts with 1 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper. Spread cut-side down on one half of the pan. Roast 10 min.
3. Whisk glaze: maple syrup, Dijon, 1 tsp olive oil, garlic, cider vinegar, smoked paprika, ¼ tsp salt.
4. Pat salmon dry, season with remaining salt and pepper. Pull the pan out, push sprouts aside, add salmon. Brush salmon generously with glaze.
5. Roast 12–14 min more until salmon flakes (internal 125–130°F) and sprouts are crisp-edged.
6. Spoon any pan glaze over the salmon to serve.

## Notes
- A 2-min broil at the end caramelizes the glaze — watch closely so the maple doesn't scorch.
- Swap sprouts for broccoli or green beans if preferred; same roast time.
