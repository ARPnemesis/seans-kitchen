# Honey Mustard Pork Tenderloin & Green Beans
*American comfort · Weeknight · 30 min · ~40g protein · ~430 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb pork tenderloin
- 12 oz green beans, trimmed
- 1 lb baby/new potatoes, halved
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper

## Method
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper; roast on a sheet pan 15 min.
2. Whisk Dijon, honey, garlic, paprika, and 1 tbsp olive oil. Pat pork dry, season with salt and pepper, brush with half the glaze.
3. Push potatoes to one side; add pork and green beans (tossed with a little oil and salt) to the pan.
4. Roast 15–18 min until pork reaches 145°F internal. Brush pork with remaining glaze in the last 5 min.
5. Rest pork 5 min, slice, and serve with the beans and potatoes.

## Notes
- Don't overcook — pull at 145°F and rest; it stays juicy.
- One sheet pan, one cleanup.
