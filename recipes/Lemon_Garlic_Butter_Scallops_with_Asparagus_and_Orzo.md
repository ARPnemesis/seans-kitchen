# Lemon-Garlic Butter Scallops with Asparagus & Orzo
*Coastal/Mediterranean · Weeknight · 25 min · ~45g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb large sea scallops, patted very dry
- 3/4 cup dry orzo
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon (zested and juiced)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Chopped parsley, for garnish
- Grated parmesan, optional
- Salt, pepper

## Method
1. Cook orzo in salted boiling water per package directions; drain, reserving 1/4 cup pasta water.
2. Steam or sauté asparagus 4–5 min until tender-crisp; set aside.
3. Pat scallops very dry and season with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a skillet over high heat until shimmering. Sear scallops in batches (don't crowd the pan), 2 min per side, until golden and just opaque. Set aside.
4. Lower heat to medium, melt butter, add garlic, sauté 30 seconds. Add lemon zest, lemon juice, and a splash of reserved pasta water.
5. Toss orzo and asparagus in the lemon-garlic butter. Plate, top with scallops, and garnish with parsley and parmesan if using.

## Notes
Don't overcrowd the pan when searing scallops — cook in batches so they get a proper golden crust instead of steaming.
