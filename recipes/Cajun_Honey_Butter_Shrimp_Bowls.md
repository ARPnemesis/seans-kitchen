# Cajun Honey-Butter Shrimp Bowls
*Cajun/Creole · Weeknight · 25 min · ~40g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 cup jasmine rice (dry)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp **honey**
- 1 tbsp **Cajun seasoning**
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley (optional garnish)

## Method
1. Cook jasmine rice: 1 cup rice + 1½ cups water, covered, 15 min; rest 5 min.
2. Pat shrimp dry; toss with Cajun seasoning, ½ tsp salt, and ¼ tsp pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add bell pepper; cook 3 min until softened. Push aside.
4. Add shrimp in a single layer; sear 1½–2 min per side until pink and opaque. Add garlic in the last 30 seconds.
5. Lower heat to medium. Add butter and honey; swirl until melted into a glossy sauce coating the shrimp. Finish with lemon juice.
6. Serve over rice, topped with green onions and parsley.

## Notes
- Don't overcook shrimp — they're done the moment they curl into a loose "C."
- Cajun blends vary in salt; taste before adding the full teaspoon.
- The honey-butter glaze plays sweet against the Cajun heat — adjust honey to taste.
