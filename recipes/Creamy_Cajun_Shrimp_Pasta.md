# Creamy Cajun Shrimp Pasta
*Cajun/American · Weeknight · 25 min · ~42g protein · ~560 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 6 oz penne (or short pasta of choice)
- 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning (divided)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- ½ yellow onion, sliced
- ¾ cup chicken stock
- ⅔ cup Greek yogurt
- ⅓ cup grated parmesan
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Boil pasta in salted water to al dente; reserve ½ cup pasta water, drain.
2. Toss shrimp with 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning. Sear in olive oil over medium-high, 1–2 min per side; remove.
3. In the same pan, melt butter; soften bell pepper and onion 3–4 min. Add garlic and remaining Cajun seasoning, 30 sec.
4. Pour in chicken stock, scrape the pan, simmer 2 min. Reduce heat to LOW.
5. Off the boil, whisk in Greek yogurt and parmesan until smooth (high heat splits yogurt — keep it gentle).
6. Toss in pasta and shrimp; loosen with pasta water as needed. Finish with lemon juice and parsley.

## Notes
- Greek yogurt instead of heavy cream — the tangy swap that made Butter Chicken a 5★.
- Temper the yogurt (low heat, off boil) or it will curdle.
- Store-bought Cajun blends vary in salt — taste before adding more.
