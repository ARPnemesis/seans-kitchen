# Honey Mustard Pork Tenderloin & Green Beans
*American comfort · Weeknight · 30 min · ~40g protein · ~430 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 pork tenderloin (~1 lb)
- 12 oz green beans, trimmed
- 1 lb baby/Yukon potatoes, halved
- Glaze: 2 tbsp Dijon mustard + 2 tbsp honey (pantry) + 1 tbsp olive oil (pantry)
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika (pantry)
- Olive oil (pantry)

## Method
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper; spread on a sheet pan and roast 12 min.
2. Pat pork dry, season all over with salt, pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika. Whisk the glaze.
3. Push potatoes aside, add pork and green beans (green beans tossed in a little oil and salt) to the pan. Brush pork with half the glaze.
4. Roast 16–18 min until pork hits 145°F internal. Brush with remaining glaze in the last 5 min.
5. Rest pork 5 min, slice, and serve with the beans and potatoes.

## Notes
- 145°F with a 5-minute rest gives juicy, slightly pink pork — don't overcook it.
- One sheet pan, minimal cleanup.
