# Weeknight Butter Chicken
*Indian · Weeknight · 30 min · ~45g protein · ~560 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- ½ cup Greek yogurt (¼ cup for marinade, ¼ cup stirred into sauce)
- 1 can (14–15 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin (pantry)
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (pantry)
- 2 naan flatbreads
- 1 cup cooked rice (pantry), to serve
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Salt to taste

## Method
1. Toss chicken with ¼ cup yogurt, 1 tsp garam masala, and the turmeric. Let sit while you prep (10 min if you have it).
2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium-high. Add ginger and garlic, cook 30 sec. Add chicken and sear 4–5 min until browned.
3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, remaining garam masala, and cumin. Simmer 10–12 min until chicken is cooked and sauce thickens.
4. Off heat, stir in remaining ¼ cup yogurt. Season with salt. Finish with cilantro.
5. Serve over rice with warm naan.

## Notes
- Stir the yogurt in off the heat so it doesn't split.
- Greek yogurt keeps this lighter than the usual heavy cream while staying rich.
