# Thai Red Curry Ground Turkey with Green Beans & Jasmine Rice

*Thai · weeknight · 30 min · ~43g protein · ~565 cal*

**Reheat rating: excellent.** Coconut-milk curries thicken overnight and reheat without breaking — the opposite failure mode from a yogurt sauce.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

- 1 lb ground turkey (93/7)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 shallot, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 12 oz fresh green beans, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced thin
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 lime, juiced, plus wedges
- 1 bunch Thai basil (regular basil is fine), leaves torn
- ½ tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. **Rice.** Rinse the jasmine rice until the water runs mostly clear. Cook with 1½ cups water — boil, cover, low, 15 minutes, rest 5 off heat.
2. **Brown the turkey.** Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high. Add the ground turkey and break it up, spreading it out and leaving it alone for 2–3 minutes to get real color before stirring. Cook 6–7 minutes total. Push to one side.
3. **Bloom the paste.** Add the shallot, garlic, and ginger to the clear side and cook 1 minute. Add the curry paste and fry it 60–90 seconds, stirring it into the fat — this step is what separates a good curry from a flat one. Stir everything together.
4. **Sauce.** Pour in the coconut milk, fish sauce, soy sauce, and brown sugar. Bring to a gentle simmer.
5. **Vegetables.** Add the green beans and bell pepper. Simmer uncovered 8–10 minutes, until the beans are tender-crisp and the sauce has reduced enough to coat a spoon.
6. **Finish.** Off heat, stir in the lime juice and most of the basil. Taste — it should read salty, sweet, sour, and hot in that order. Serve over jasmine rice with the remaining basil and a lime wedge.

## Notes

- **Curry paste brands vary wildly in heat.** Start at 2 tbsp, taste after the coconut milk goes in, add the third if it needs it.
- Shake the coconut milk can hard before opening, or scoop the thick cream out first and fry it with the paste for 2 minutes — the traditional and better method if you have the extra minute.
- Fish sauce is doing the salt here. Don't add salt until you've tasted the finished sauce.
- **Day two:** it thickens in the fridge. Reheat gently with a splash of water; the green beans stay good. Reheat the rice separately with a wet paper towel over it.
