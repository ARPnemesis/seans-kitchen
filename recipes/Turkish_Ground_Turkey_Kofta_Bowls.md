# Turkish Ground Turkey Kofta Bowls with Tahini Drizzle
*Turkish · Weeknight · 30 min · ~38g protein · ~510 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb ground turkey
- ¼ cup yellow onion, finely minced (squeeze out excess water)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp ground allspice
- Pinch of cayenne
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil, for cooking
- **Tahini drizzle:** 3 tbsp tahini, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 clove garlic minced, water to thin, pinch of salt
- 1½ cups cooked rice
- 1 cup diced cucumber and tomato
- ¼ cup pickled red onion (optional)

## Method
1. In a bowl, combine ground turkey, minced onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, smoked paprika, cinnamon, allspice, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Mix gently — don't overwork. Form into 8 small oblong koftas.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook koftas 4–5 min per side until browned and cooked through (165°F internal).
3. Whisk tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and salt together, adding water 1 tbsp at a time until pourable.
4. Serve koftas over rice with cucumber, tomato, and pickled onion. Drizzle with tahini sauce.

## Notes
- Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 12 min, or microwave, covered, 90 sec.
- Yield: ~2 meals (1 dinner + 1 lunch).
- Sanctioned fallback: no allspice → extra pinch of cinnamon does the job.
- Vegetable ceiling: cucumber + tomato combined kept under 8 oz.
- Zero Greek yogurt — tahini stands in for the usual yogurt sauce this week, per the "no more than one yogurt-forward sauce per week" guidance (this dish uses none at all).
- New cuisine for the kitchen — Turkish has no prior rating data.
