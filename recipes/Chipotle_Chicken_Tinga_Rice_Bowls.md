# Chipotle Chicken Tinga Rice Bowls

*Mexican · weeknight · 35 min · ~45g protein · ~580 cal*

**Reheat rating: excellent.** Shredded chicken in a smoky tomato-chipotle sauce is genuinely better on day two. Store the rice and the tinga together; add avocado and cotija fresh at lunch.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Tinga**
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 (14.5 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 2–3 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced, + 1 tbsp of the adobo sauce
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Bowls**
- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 limes (juice for the rice, wedges to serve)
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 avocado, sliced
- ⅓ cup cotija cheese, crumbled (queso fresco works)

## Method

1. **Rice first.** Rinse the rice, then cook with 2 cups water and ½ tsp salt — bring to a boil, cover, drop to low, 18 minutes, rest 5 off heat. Fluff with the juice of 1 lime and half the cilantro.
2. **Sear the chicken.** Pat the thighs dry, salt and pepper both sides. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until it shimmers. Sear the thighs 4 minutes per side until well browned — they do not need to be cooked through yet. Move to a plate.
3. **Build the sauce.** Drop to medium. Add the sliced onion to the same pan and cook 5 minutes until soft and picking up the fond. Add the garlic, cumin, oregano, and smoked paprika, stir 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the tomato paste and cook it 1 minute — it should darken.
4. **Simmer.** Add the diced tomatoes, minced chipotles + adobo, and chicken broth. Scrape the bottom of the pan clean. Return the chicken and any juices, nestling it into the sauce. Cover and simmer on low 15 minutes.
5. **Shred.** Pull the thighs onto a board, shred with two forks, and return to the pan. Stir to coat and simmer uncovered 3–4 more minutes until the sauce clings. Taste for salt.
6. **Warm the beans** in a small pot with a splash of their liquid and a pinch of cumin.
7. **Assemble.** Cilantro-lime rice, black beans, a heap of tinga, avocado, cotija, remaining cilantro, lime wedge.

## Notes

- **Heat control:** 2 chipotles is medium-warm, 3 is assertive. The adobo sauce carries as much heat as the pepper — hold some back and add at the end if you're unsure.
- Chicken thighs are the right call here; breasts go stringy when shredded into an acidic sauce.
- Leftover chipotles freeze well — spoon them onto a sheet of parchment in single-pepper blobs, freeze, bag.
- **Day two:** reheat the tinga and rice together in a covered pan with a splash of water. Slice fresh avocado at lunch — don't pre-slice it.
