# Nigerian-Inspired Suya-Spiced Chicken Thighs with Peanut Dipping Sauce
*West African (Nigerian) · Weeknight · 35 min · ~42g protein · ~540 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1¼ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp neutral oil, divided
- 2 tbsp roasted peanuts, finely ground (or 2 tbsp natural peanut butter, see fallback)
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 bouillon cube, crushed (or ½ tsp salt if unavailable)
- **Peanut dipping sauce:** 3 tbsp natural peanut butter, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp lime juice, water to thin
- 1½ cups cooked jasmine rice
- 1 cup sliced bell peppers and red onion, for serving

## Method
1. Pat chicken thighs dry. In a bowl, mix ground peanuts, smoked paprika, cayenne, ginger, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and crushed bouillon with 1 tbsp oil to form a paste (the suya spice rub).
2. Coat chicken thoroughly in the rub. Marinate at least 20 min (up to overnight for best flavor).
3. Heat remaining 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high, or preheat oven to 400°F. Sear chicken 5–6 min per side (skillet) or bake 25–30 min until internal temp hits 165°F.
4. Whisk peanut dipping sauce ingredients together, thinning with water 1 tbsp at a time to a drizzle consistency.
5. Slice chicken, serve over rice with peppers and onion, drizzled with peanut sauce.

## Notes
- Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 12–15 min, or microwave, covered, 90 sec.
- Yield: ~2 meals (1 dinner + 1 lunch).
- Sanctioned fallback: no ground peanuts for the rub → skip, the peanut flavor still comes through in the dipping sauce. No bouillon cube → ½ tsp salt.
- Vegetable ceiling: bell pepper + onion combined kept under 8 oz.
- Zero Greek yogurt.
- New cuisine for the kitchen — Nigerian/West African has no prior rating data.
