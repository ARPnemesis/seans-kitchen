# Miso-Glazed Cod with Bok Choy & Rice
*Japanese / Asian · weeknight · ~25 min · ~38g protein · ~470 cal*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 2 cod fillets (~6 oz each; frozen is fine, thawed)
- 2 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 2 baby bok choy, halved lengthwise
- 1 tbsp olive or avocado oil
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 cup cooked white rice (for the base)
- 1/4 tsp salt

## Method
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Whisk miso, rice vinegar, soy sauce, honey, ginger, and garlic.
2. Pat cod dry, set on a lined sheet pan, and brush generously with the miso glaze.
3. Add bok choy alongside; drizzle with oil and a pinch of salt.
4. Roast 12–15 min until the cod flakes easily and the glaze caramelizes.
5. Serve over rice; scatter green onion and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- **Check you have enough rice vinegar, soy sauce, honey, and sesame seeds** — these run out fast and were missed in the past.
- Don't overcook the cod; pull it the moment it flakes.
- Shares ginger, garlic, green onion, soy, sesame, and rice with the week.
