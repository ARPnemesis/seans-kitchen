# Korean Beef Bulgogi Bowls
*Korean · Weeknight · 30 min · ~40g protein · ~560 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb thinly sliced ribeye or sirloin (or flank steak, sliced thin against the grain)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 Asian pear or ½ apple, grated (tenderizer — sub 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tsp sugar if unavailable)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, for cooking
- 2 scallions, sliced (plus extra for garnish)
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1½ cups cooked jasmine rice
- 1 cup shredded carrot
- 1 cup thinly sliced cucumber

## Method
1. Whisk soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and grated pear/apple in a bowl. Add beef, toss to coat, marinate 15 min (up to overnight).
2. Heat neutral oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Working in batches, sear beef 2–3 min per side until caramelized and just cooked through — don't crowd the pan.
3. Return all beef to the pan, add scallions, toss 30 sec.
4. Serve over rice with shredded carrot and cucumber alongside. Top with sesame seeds and extra scallion.

## Notes
- Reheat: skillet over medium-high 3–4 min (best for caramelization), or microwave, covered, 90 sec.
- Yield: ~2 meals (1 dinner + 1 lunch).
- Sanctioned fallback: no Asian pear → 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tsp sugar in the marinade does the same tenderizing job.
- Vegetable ceiling: carrot and cucumber are both light, well under the 8 oz single-vegetable ceiling.
- Zero Greek yogurt.
- RECYCLE — Critic-flagged 4–5★ favorite ("easy and delicious"), clear of the no-repeat window.
