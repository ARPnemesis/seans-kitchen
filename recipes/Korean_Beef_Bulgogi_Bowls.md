# Korean Beef Bulgogi Bowls
*Korean · Weeknight · 30 min (+15 min marinade) · ~43g protein · ~560 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb flank or sirloin steak, thinly sliced against the grain
- 1 cup jasmine rice (dry)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp **soy sauce**
- 2 tbsp **brown sugar** (or 1 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp brown sugar)
- 1 tbsp **toasted sesame oil**
- 1 tsp **rice vinegar**
- 1 tsp gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 1 tbsp **sesame seeds**
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (for searing)
- 1 cucumber, sliced (quick side)
- ½ tsp salt

## Method
1. Whisk marinade: soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, rice vinegar, gochujang, garlic, ginger.
2. Toss sliced steak in half the marinade; rest 15 min (reserve the rest for finishing).
3. Cook jasmine rice: 1 cup rice + 1½ cups water, covered, 15 min; rest 5 min.
4. Heat neutral oil in a skillet over high. Sear steak in a single layer 1–2 min per side until caramelized — work in batches so it sears, not steams.
5. Add reserved marinade to the pan; toss 1 min until glossy.
6. Serve steak over rice, topped with green onions and sesame seeds. Side of sliced cucumber.

## Notes
- Freeze the steak 20 min before slicing — it firms up for paper-thin cuts.
- High heat and a dry pan are key to the bulgogi char; don't crowd the skillet.
- Gochujang adds gentle heat; leave it out for a milder, sweeter bowl.
