# Harissa Chicken & Chickpea Sheet-Pan Bowls
*Middle Eastern · weeknight · ~30 min · ~46g protein · ~520 cal*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into strips
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp harissa paste (or 1 tbsp harissa powder)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup cooked white or brown rice (for the base)
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1/2 lemon (juice)
- 2 tbsp crumbled feta
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

## Method
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan.
2. Toss chicken, chickpeas, bell pepper, and onion with harissa, olive oil, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread on the pan in one layer.
3. Roast 22–25 min until chicken reaches 165°F and the edges char.
4. Stir together Greek yogurt, grated garlic, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt for the drizzle.
5. Build bowls over rice; top with feta, parsley, and the garlicky yogurt.

## Notes
- Chicken thighs usually come in ~1.3–1.5 lb packs — use the extra for lunch.
- Harissa heat varies; start with less if you're heat-shy.
- Shares Greek yogurt, garlic, red onion, bell pepper, lemon, and feta with the rest of the week.
