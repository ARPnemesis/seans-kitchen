# Thai Basil Beef (Pad Krapow)
*Thai · Weeknight · 20 min · ~40g protein · ~510 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- Ground beef, 90/10, 1 lb
- Thai basil (or regular basil), 1 cup leaves, loosely packed
- Bell pepper, 1, thinly sliced
- Yellow onion, 1/2, sliced
- Garlic, 4 cloves, minced
- Soy sauce, 2 tbsp
- Oyster sauce, 1.5 tbsp
- Fish sauce, 1 tbsp
- Brown sugar, 1 tsp
- Lime, 1, cut into wedges
- Red chili flakes, 1/2 tsp (to taste)
- Neutral oil, 1 tbsp
- Eggs, 2 (for fried-egg topping)
- Cooked jasmine rice, ~1.5 cups

## Method
1. Mix sauce: soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, brown sugar, 1 tbsp water.
2. Heat oil over high. Add garlic and chili flakes, 15 sec. Add beef; brown and break up, 4–5 min.
3. Add onion and bell pepper, stir-fry 2–3 min. Pour in sauce, toss 1 min until glossy.
4. Off heat, fold in basil until just wilted. Serve over rice with a fried egg and lime wedges.

## Notes
- One of the fastest meals of the week — have rice going before you start.
- Eggs are covered by the weekly staples dozen.
- Shares bell pepper, onion, lime, and rice with other dishes this week.
