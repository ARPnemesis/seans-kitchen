# Chimichurri Flank Steak with Charred Corn & Tomato Salad
*Argentinian/Latin · Weekend · 40 min · ~46g protein · ~560 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb flank steak
- 1 cup fresh parsley leaves, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1/3 cup olive oil (for chimichurri) + 1 tbsp for searing
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 ears corn, shucked (or 1 1/2 cups frozen corn, charred in a dry skillet)
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 lime, juiced
- Salt, black pepper

## Method
1. Make the chimichurri: stir together parsley, garlic, red wine vinegar, 1/3 cup olive oil, chili flakes, oregano, and 1/2 tsp salt. Let it sit while everything else cooks (it gets better).
2. Pat the steak dry, season generously with salt and pepper, and let it come toward room temp ~15 min.
3. Grill or sear the corn in a hot dry pan, turning, until charred in spots, ~8 min. Cut kernels off the cob.
4. Toss corn with cherry tomatoes, red onion, lime juice, a drizzle of olive oil, and salt. Set the salad aside.
5. Grill or pan-sear the steak over high heat, 4–5 min per side for medium-rare (130°F). Rest 10 minutes — don't skip this on flank.
6. Slice thin AGAINST the grain, spoon chimichurri generously over the top, and serve with the corn-tomato salad.

## Notes
- Flank is only tender if sliced thin against the grain after a real rest.
- Leftover chimichurri is great on eggs or sandwiches all week.
- This is grilled-steak-with-salad, not a stir-fry — a deliberately different beef format from Lomo Saltado.
