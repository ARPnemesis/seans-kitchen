# Cuban Mojo Pork Chops with Black Beans & Rice
*Cuban/Latin · Weeknight · 30 min · ~42g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 2 boneless pork chops (~0.75–1 lb total)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil (plus more for searing)
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 cup dry white rice
- 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 small onion, diced
- Chopped cilantro, for garnish
- Salt, pepper

## Method
1. Whisk garlic, orange juice, lime juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper for the mojo marinade. Marinate pork chops at least 15 min (up to overnight in the fridge).
2. Cook rice according to package directions.
3. Heat a skillet over medium-high with a little olive oil. Sear pork chops 4–5 min per side until internal temp hits 145°F. Rest 5 min, then slice.
4. In the same skillet, sauté onion 2 min, add black beans, and warm through, seasoning with salt and pepper.
5. Serve sliced pork over rice and beans, spoon any pan juices over top, and garnish with cilantro.

## Notes
The longer the pork marinates, the brighter the mojo flavor — overnight is ideal if you're prepping ahead.
