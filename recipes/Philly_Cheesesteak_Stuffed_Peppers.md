# Philly Cheesesteak Stuffed Peppers

*American comfort · weeknight · 30 min · ~44g protein · ~470 cal*

## Ingredients (2 servings)

- 2 large green bell peppers
- 1 lb ground beef (90/10)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 4 slices provolone cheese
- 2 tbsp beef broth (or water)
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Halve the bell peppers lengthwise and pull out the seeds and ribs. Set them cut-side up in a baking dish, rub lightly with olive oil, and roast 10 minutes while you build the filling.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the onion and mushrooms and cook 6–7 minutes until the mushrooms have given up their water and started to brown. Add the garlic for the last 30 seconds.
3. Push the vegetables aside, add the ground beef, and break it up. Cook 6–8 minutes until browned through. Drain excess fat if there's more than a tablespoon.
4. Stir in the Worcestershire, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and beef broth. Simmer 2 minutes until it's glossy, not soupy.
5. Pull the peppers from the oven. Pack the filling into each half. Lay a slice of provolone over each.
6. Return to the oven 8–10 minutes until the cheese is melted and blistered in spots. Finish with parsley.

## Notes
- Green peppers are the traditional Philly choice, but red or orange are sweeter and just as good.
- Ground beef stands in for shaved ribeye here — it's the weeknight version. If you want the real thing, freeze a small sirloin 20 minutes and shave it thin.
- Provolone is correct. White American melts even better; skip cheddar, which fights the Worcestershire.
