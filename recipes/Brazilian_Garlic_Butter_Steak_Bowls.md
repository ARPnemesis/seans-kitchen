# Brazilian Garlic Butter Steak Bowls
*Brazilian · Weeknight · 25 min · ~40g protein · ~580 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 12 oz flat-iron or sirloin steak, sliced thin against the grain
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup jasmine or long-grain rice, cooked
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 avocado, sliced
- ¼ cup chopped parsley
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Flaky sea salt
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Cook rice per package instructions.
2. Pat steak dry, season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over high heat. Sear steak 2–3 min per side for medium-rare (or to preference). Rest 5 min, then slice thin against the grain.
4. Reduce heat to low; melt butter with garlic, swirling until lightly golden, ~2–3 min. Do not let it brown.
5. Build bowls: rice, sliced steak, cherry tomatoes, avocado. Spoon garlic butter over the top, finish with parsley, green onions, flaky salt, and a lemon wedge.

## Notes
- Reheat: skillet over medium, 3–4 min, gently (steak is best reheated low and slow, not microwaved hot, to avoid overcooking) — or microwave, covered, 90 sec at 50% power.
- Yield: 2 dinners — steak doesn't hold past day 2 as well as a braise; eat within 2 days.
