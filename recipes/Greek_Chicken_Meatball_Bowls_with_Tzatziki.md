# Greek Chicken Meatball Bowls with Tzatziki
*Greek/Mediterranean · Weeknight · 30 min · ~43g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb ground chicken
- ⅓ cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 4 cloves garlic, minced (divided)
- 1½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ½ red onion, finely diced (half for meatballs, half for topping)
- 1 lemon (zest + juice)
- ¾ cup couscous (dry)
- 1 cup chicken stock (to cook couscous)
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 cucumber (half grated for tzatziki, half diced for the bowl)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- ¼ cup crumbled feta
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp dried dill (or 1 tbsp fresh)
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Mix ground chicken, panko, egg, half the garlic, oregano, cumin, half the red onion, lemon zest, ¾ tsp salt, and pepper. Roll into ~12 meatballs.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Brown meatballs 8–10 min, turning, until cooked through (165°F).
3. Meanwhile, bring chicken stock to a boil, stir in couscous, cover, off heat 5 min; fluff.
4. Tzatziki: grate half the cucumber, squeeze dry, stir into Greek yogurt with remaining garlic, dill, half the lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, and salt.
5. Build bowls: couscous, meatballs, diced cucumber, cherry tomatoes, remaining red onion, feta. Big spoonful of tzatziki, squeeze of remaining lemon.

## Notes
- Wet hands keep ground chicken from sticking while rolling.
- Squeezing the grated cucumber dry is the difference between tzatziki and soup.
- Tangy yogurt sauce — the same style that made the Butter Chicken a 5★ hit.
