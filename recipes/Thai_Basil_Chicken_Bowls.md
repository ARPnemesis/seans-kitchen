# Thai Basil Chicken Bowls (Pad Krapow Gai)
*Thai · Weeknight · 25 min · ~44g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb ground chicken (or finely chopped chicken thighs)
- 1 cup jasmine rice (dry)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 Thai chiles or 1 jalapeño, minced (adjust to heat preference)
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 1 cup Thai basil leaves (sub regular basil if unavailable)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (avocado or vegetable)
- 2 eggs (for fried-egg topping)
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Method
1. Cook jasmine rice: rinse, then simmer 1 cup rice with 1½ cups water, covered, 15 min; rest 5 min.
2. Stir together soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, and brown sugar; set aside.
3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add garlic and chiles; stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add ground chicken. Break up and cook 5–6 min until browned and cooked through.
5. Pour in the sauce; toss to coat and cook 1–2 min until glossy.
6. Kill the heat, fold in green onions and Thai basil until just wilted.
7. In a separate pan, fry the eggs in a little oil until edges are crisp and yolks still runny.
8. Serve chicken over rice, topped with a fried egg.

## Notes
- Authentic pad krapow leans hot and savory — the fried egg and rice balance the heat.
- Thai basil has an anise note; regular basil works in a pinch but tastes milder.
- Fish sauce and oyster sauce both carry salt — taste before adding extra.
