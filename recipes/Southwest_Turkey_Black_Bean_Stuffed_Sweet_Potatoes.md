# Southwest Turkey & Black Bean Stuffed Sweet Potatoes
*Southwest/Tex-Mex · Weeknight · 30 min · ~40g protein · ~540 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 2 medium sweet potatoes
- 0.75 lb ground turkey
- 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup corn kernels (frozen or canned)
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar or Mexican-blend cheese
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 small onion, diced
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 lime
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper
- Optional: sour cream or Greek yogurt, for topping

## Method
1. Pierce sweet potatoes with a fork; microwave 8–10 min until tender (or bake at 425°F for ~40 min if you have time).
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sauté onion 2–3 min, then garlic 30 seconds.
3. Add ground turkey, breaking it up as it browns, ~6–7 min. Season with chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
4. Stir in black beans and corn; cook 2–3 min until warmed through.
5. Split the sweet potatoes open, fluff the flesh slightly. Top with the turkey mixture, cheese, cilantro, a squeeze of lime, and a dollop of yogurt or sour cream if using.

## Notes
Microwaving the sweet potatoes keeps this weeknight-fast; oven-roasting deepens the flavor if you have the extra 15–20 minutes.
