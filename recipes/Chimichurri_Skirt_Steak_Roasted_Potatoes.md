# Chimichurri Skirt Steak & Roasted Potatoes
*Argentine · Weekend · 35 min · ~40g protein · ~540 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- Skirt steak, 1 lb
- Baby potatoes, 1.5 lb, halved
- Fresh parsley, 1 cup packed, finely chopped
- Cilantro, 1/4 cup (optional, from the shared bunch)
- Garlic, 3 cloves, minced
- Red wine vinegar, 2 tbsp
- Dried oregano, 1 tsp
- Red pepper flakes, 1/2 tsp
- Olive oil, 1/2 cup (for chimichurri) + 1 tbsp (potatoes)
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper; roast 25–30 min, flipping once, until golden.
2. Chimichurri: stir parsley, cilantro, garlic, vinegar, oregano, red pepper flakes, and 1/2 cup olive oil with a good pinch of salt. Let sit 10 min.
3. Pat steak dry, season well. Sear in a hot cast-iron skillet 2–4 min per side for medium-rare. Rest 10 min.
4. Slice steak against the grain, spoon chimichurri over, serve with the roasted potatoes.

## Notes
- Weekend dish — the rest and the chimichurri reward a little patience.
- Slicing against the grain is what keeps skirt steak tender.
- Shares cilantro, garlic, and potatoes with other dishes this week.
