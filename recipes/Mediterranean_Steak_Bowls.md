# Mediterranean Steak Bowls
*Mediterranean / global gourmet · Weekend · 40 min · ~44g protein · ~580 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- ¾ lb sirloin or flat-iron steak
- ¾ cup dry couscous (makes ~2 cups cooked)
- 1 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes
- ½ cup crumbled feta
- Yogurt sauce: ⅓ cup Greek yogurt + ½ cucumber grated + 1 clove garlic + squeeze of lemon
- 1 lemon
- Olive oil, dried oregano, salt, pepper, cumin (pantry)

## Method
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss zucchini, bell pepper, and cherry tomatoes with olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Roast 20–25 min.
2. Cook couscous per package (pour boiling water over, cover, fluff after 5 min).
3. Season steak with salt, pepper, cumin. Sear in a hot oiled skillet 3–4 min per side for medium; rest 5 min, then slice against the grain.
4. Stir the yogurt sauce together.
5. Build bowls: couscous, roasted veg, sliced steak, feta. Drizzle with yogurt sauce and a squeeze of lemon.

## Notes
- Resting the steak before slicing keeps it juicy; slice against the grain for tenderness.
- Grate the cucumber and squeeze out excess water so the yogurt sauce stays thick.
