# Philly Cheesesteak Hoagies

*American comfort · weeknight · 30 min · ~48g protein · ~625 cal per serving*

> 🔥 **This is the dish you asked for by name.** The stuffed-pepper version scored 5★ back on 07-27 and your note asked for the filling on a hoagie "at some point." This is that, built properly: shaved steak instead of ground beef, provolone melted into the meat in the pan, and a toasted roll.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Filling**
- 1 lb beef shaved steak (sold as "shaved steak" or "sandwich steak" near the ground beef) — **if unavailable: 1 lb sirloin or ribeye, frozen 20 min then sliced paper-thin against the grain**
- 1 large yellow onion, halved and sliced thin
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced thin
- 6 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced *(optional but good — keep to 6 oz; more and it waters the pan)*
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp neutral oil, divided
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

**To finish**
- 6 slices provolone (or white American)
- 2 hoagie rolls (6–8 inch), soft-crumbed
- 2 tbsp butter, softened
- 2 tbsp mayo *(optional, for the roll)*

## Method

1. **Season the peppers and onions BEFORE they cook.** Toss the sliced onion and bell pepper with 1/4 tsp of the salt and a few grinds of pepper in a bowl and let them sit while the pan heats. *(Your note from 07-27: unseasoned peppers "kind of dulled the Philly cheesesteak flavor." This is the fix — salt drawn in early seasons them through instead of sitting on the surface.)*
2. **Cook the vegetables.** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the onion, pepper and mushrooms and cook 7–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the onions are soft and browning at the edges and the mushrooms have given up their water. Garlic in for the last 30 seconds. Scrape it all onto a plate.
3. **Sear the steak hard.** Wipe the pan, return it to high heat with the remaining 1 tbsp oil, and get it properly hot. Add the shaved steak in a single layer and **leave it alone for 90 seconds** to take color. Then break it up with the edge of a spatula and cook 2–3 minutes more until no pink remains. Shaved steak cooks fast — this is a 4-minute job, not a 10-minute one.
4. **Season and combine.** Add the Worcestershire, garlic powder, onion powder, remaining 1/2 tsp salt and black pepper. Return the vegetables to the pan and toss everything together, chopping through the meat a few more times with the spatula.
5. **Melt the cheese into the meat.** Divide the filling into two piles in the pan. Lay 3 slices of provolone over each pile, drop the heat to low, cover the pan for 60–90 seconds until the cheese is fully melted, then fold it through.
6. **Toast the rolls.** Split the hoagies 3/4 of the way through — hinged, not separated. Butter the cut faces and toast in a dry skillet or under the broiler 1–2 minutes until golden. Spread with mayo if using.
7. **Build.** Slide a spatula under each pile and transfer it whole into a roll. Press the top down. Eat immediately.

## Notes

- **Reheat: 350°F oven, 12–15 min, wrapped loosely in foil.** This is exactly how you reheated the stuffed-pepper version ("just threw right back in the oven") and it's the right call here too. **Do not microwave the assembled sandwich** — the roll goes to rubber. If you're packing lunch, store the *filling* and the *roll* separately, reheat the filling covered in the microwave 2 min, and toast the roll fresh.
- **Provolone is the correct cheese.** White American melts even better if you find it. Skip cheddar — it fights the Worcestershire.
- Split the roll only 3/4 of the way so the filling has a hinge to sit in rather than sliding out the back.
- If the shaved steak is packaged frozen, that's fine and often better — thaw it in the fridge overnight before cooking.
