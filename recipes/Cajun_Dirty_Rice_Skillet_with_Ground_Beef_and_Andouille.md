# Cajun Dirty Rice Skillet with Ground Beef & Andouille

*Cajun / Creole · weeknight · 40 min · ~44g protein · ~590 cal · **yield: 3 meals** (1 dinner + 2 lunches)*

> 🌶️ **This is the deliberate Cajun retry.** Cajun has two data points in this kitchen and both failed for reasons that had nothing to do with the seasoning — a Greek-yogurt cream sauce (1★) and a shrimp reheat (3★). The Critic's instruction was explicit: *try a Cajun build on a reheat-tolerant protein before writing the cuisine off.* Ground beef and smoked sausage in a one-pot rice is about as reheat-tolerant as this kitchen gets. **No dairy anywhere in this dish. No shrimp.**

## Ingredients (2 servings — makes 3 meals)

**Skillet**
- ¾ lb 85/15 ground beef
- 6 oz andouille sausage, halved lengthwise and sliced into half-moons — *sanctioned fallback: any smoked sausage or kielbasa; the dish still works, it just loses a little heat*
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced — **season it with a pinch of salt and pepper as it goes into the pan, not after** (your standing request; unseasoned peppers dulled the cheesesteak)
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup long-grain white rice, rinsed
- 2 cups chicken stock
- 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning (Tony Chachere's or Slap Ya Mama) — *sanctioned fallback: 1 tsp smoked paprika + ½ tsp garlic powder + ½ tsp onion powder + ½ tsp dried thyme + ¼ tsp cayenne*
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp **Worcestershire sauce**
- 1 bay leaf
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp salt (hold back if your Cajun blend is salty — most are)

**To finish**
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Hot sauce at the table

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high. Brown the andouille 3–4 minutes until the edges crisp and the fat renders. Scoop it out and leave the fat behind.
2. Add the ground beef and break it up. Cook 5–6 minutes until well browned — let it sit undisturbed for the first two minutes so it crusts rather than steams. Drain off the excess fat if there's more than a tablespoon.
3. Add the onion, **seasoned** bell pepper, and celery. Cook 5 minutes until softened. Add the garlic, Cajun seasoning, thyme, and tomato paste; stir constantly for 1 minute — you want the tomato paste to darken and the spices to bloom in the fat.
4. Stir in the rinsed rice and coat it in the fat and spice. Add the stock, Worcestershire, bay leaf, pepper, salt, and the reserved sausage. Bring to a boil.
5. Cover, drop to the lowest heat, and cook **20 minutes without lifting the lid.** Then pull it off the heat and let it stand, still covered, 5 more minutes.
6. Fluff with a fork, discard the bay leaf, and fold in the green onions and parsley. Hot sauce at the table.

## Notes
- 🔥 **REHEAT: microwave, covered, 2 minutes, with a splash of water or stock stirred through first.** Dirty rice is one of the few dishes that is arguably better on day two — the spice settles into the rice overnight. Oven works too: covered dish, 350°F, 15 minutes.
- **Vegetable ceiling:** one onion, one pepper, two ribs of celery. That's the trinity and it's the correct ratio — the protein and the rice lead here.
- Perishability: ground beef is a front-half protein. Cook this Monday–Wednesday, or **freeze the beef on arrival** and thaw in the fridge the night before.
- Rinsing the rice matters — unrinsed rice turns this gluey.
