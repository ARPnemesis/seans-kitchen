# Peruvian Beef Stir-Fry (Lomo Saltado)
*Peruvian · Weekend · 40 min · ~44g protein · ~580 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 0.75–1 lb sirloin or flank steak, sliced into thin strips
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 red onion, sliced into wedges
- 1 large tomato, cut into wedges
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 jalapeño, sliced (optional, for heat)
- 1.5 cups frozen French fries
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- 3/4 cup dry white rice
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper

## Method
1. Bake or air-fry the French fries according to package directions until crispy.
2. Cook rice according to package directions.
3. Toss steak strips with soy sauce, red wine vinegar, cumin, salt, and pepper; let sit 10 min.
4. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Sear steak in batches 1–2 min until browned but still pink inside; set aside.
5. Add remaining oil to the pan, sauté onion and jalapeño 2–3 min until slightly charred. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds. Add tomato wedges, cook 1–2 min until just softened.
6. Return steak to the pan, add the crispy fries, and toss everything together quickly so the fries stay crisp. Finish with cilantro.
7. Serve immediately over rice.

## Notes
High heat and a quick toss are key — the fries should stay crisp, not go soggy in the sauce.
