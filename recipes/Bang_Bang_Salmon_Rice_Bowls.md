# Bang Bang Salmon Rice Bowls

*Asian-fusion · weeknight · 25 min · ~42g protein · ~580 cal*

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Salmon**
- 1 lb salmon fillet, skin removed, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp cornstarch

**Bang bang sauce**
- ¼ cup mayo
- 3 tbsp Thai sweet chili sauce
- 1 tbsp sriracha (up or down to taste)
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp lime juice

**Bowls**
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 lime, cut in wedges

## Method

1. Cook the rice: rinse 1 cup jasmine rice, combine with 1½ cups water, bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low 15 minutes. Rest 5 minutes off heat, then fluff.
2. Whisk all the bang bang sauce ingredients together. Taste and adjust — more sriracha for heat, more honey to round it out. Set aside.
3. Pat the salmon cubes very dry. Toss with the garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, pepper, and cornstarch until evenly coated. The cornstarch is what gives you the crust.
4. Heat the neutral oil in a nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add the salmon in a single layer — do not crowd it. Sear undisturbed 2–3 minutes, then turn and cook another 2–3 minutes until the cubes are golden on at least two sides and just cooked through.
5. Build the bowls: rice, salmon, cucumber, avocado. Drizzle generously with bang bang sauce, then scatter green onions, cilantro, and sesame seeds. Lime wedge on the side.

## Notes
- Dry salmon and a hot pan are the whole recipe. Wet fish steams instead of crisping.
- The sauce keeps a week in the fridge and is excellent on the leftover rice, on sandwiches, or as a dip for anything fried.
- Shrimp works identically if salmon isn't in the cart — same cornstarch toss, 90 seconds a side.
