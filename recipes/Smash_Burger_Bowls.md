# Smash Burger Bowls
*American comfort · Weeknight · 25 min · ~42g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 12 oz (¾ lb) 90/10 ground beef
- ½ head romaine or iceberg lettuce, chopped
- 1 Roma tomato, diced
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- ⅓ cup dill pickle chips, chopped
- ½ cup shredded cheddar
- Special sauce: 3 tbsp Greek yogurt + 1 tbsp mayo + 1 tbsp ketchup + 2 tsp yellow mustard + 1 tbsp chopped pickle
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder (pantry)
- Sesame seeds, to garnish (pantry)

## Method
1. Whisk the special sauce ingredients together; set aside.
2. Divide beef into 2 loose balls. Heat a cast-iron or heavy skillet over high until smoking. Add beef and smash each ball flat with a spatula. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
3. Sear 2–3 min until a deep crust forms, flip, top with cheddar, cook 1–2 min more. Chop into craggy pieces.
4. Build bowls: lettuce base, then beef, tomato, red onion, pickles. Drizzle with special sauce and finish with sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Cooking over high heat is what gives the smash crust — don't move the patties until the crust sets.
- Add a scoop of cooked rice or crispy potatoes to the bowl if you want it heartier.
