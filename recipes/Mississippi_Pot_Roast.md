# Mississippi Pot Roast
*American Comfort · Weekend · 8 hrs slow cooker (15 min prep) · ~40g protein · ~480 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2–3 servings)
- 2 lbs chuck roast
- 1 packet au jus gravy mix
- 1 packet ranch dressing mix
- 8–10 pepperoncini peppers + a splash of the brine
- 4 tbsp (½ stick) butter
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Season chuck roast generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
2. Place roast in the slow cooker. Sprinkle au jus packet and ranch packet evenly over the top.
3. Add pepperoncini peppers around the roast and pour in a splash of the brine.
4. Lay butter on top of the roast in pats.
5. Cover and cook on LOW for 8 hours (do not add water or open the lid mid-cook). The roast will release its own liquid.
6. Shred with two forks directly in the slow cooker and mix into the juices.
7. Serve over mashed potatoes or with crusty bread for soaking up the juices.

## Notes
- Chuck roast is the only cut that works here — don't substitute. The fat renders down completely over 8 hours.
- Leftovers reheat beautifully and make excellent sandwiches the next day.
