# Soy-Marinated Skirt Steak with Chimichurri & Sweet Potato
*Latin-Asian Fusion · Weekend · 45 min · ~45g protein · ~580 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb skirt steak
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 2 garlic cloves, minced (marinade)
- 1 large sweet potato, cubed
- Chimichurri: ½ cup fresh parsley (packed), 2 garlic cloves, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, pinch chili flakes, salt

## Method
1. Combine soy sauce, red wine vinegar, honey, and garlic. Marinate steak at least 20–30 min.
2. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potato with olive oil, salt, and smoked paprika. Roast 25–30 min, flipping halfway.
3. Make chimichurri: finely chop parsley and garlic, mix with olive oil, red wine vinegar, chili flakes, and salt.
4. Remove steak from marinade. Sear in a very hot, dry pan 2–3 min per side for medium-rare. Rest 5 min.
5. Slice against the grain, serve over sweet potatoes, drizzle chimichurri generously on top.
