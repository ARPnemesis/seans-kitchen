# Hawaiian-Style Turkey Meatballs with Pineapple Fried Rice
*Hawaiian/Pacific · Weeknight · 30 min · ~38g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb ground turkey
- ¼ cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 2 tbsp soy sauce, divided
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cups cooked, cooled rice (day-old is ideal)
- 1 cup pineapple chunks, fresh or canned in juice (drained)
- ½ cup diced bell pepper
- ¼ cup diced red onion
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- Sesame seeds, for garnish

## Method
1. **Season the bell pepper with salt and pepper before the pre-cook** (standing request).
2. Mix ground turkey, panko, egg, garlic, ginger, and 1 tbsp soy sauce. Form into 12 meatballs.
3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium. Cook meatballs 8–10 min, turning, until browned and cooked through (165°F). Remove.
4. Add sesame oil, bell pepper, and red onion to the skillet, sauté 2 min. Add rice, breaking up clumps, stir-fry 3–4 min.
5. Stir in pineapple, remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce, honey, and rice vinegar. Toss 1–2 min until heated through.
6. Return meatballs to the pan, toss to coat. Top with green onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Reheat: microwave, covered, 2 min, or skillet over medium, 4–5 min.
- Yield: 2 dinners + 1 lunch (~3 meals).
- Sanctioned fallback: no fresh pineapple → canned pineapple chunks in juice (drained, not syrup) works the same.
- Vegetable ceiling: bell pepper ½ cup diced, well under the 8 oz ceiling.
- **Ground turkey is a front-half perishable protein — place Mon–Wed; freeze on arrival if it slips later in the week.**
- New cuisine for the kitchen — Hawaiian/Pacific has no prior rating data.
- Zero Greek yogurt.
