# Harissa Braised Chicken Thighs with Chickpeas & Couscous
*North African · Weeknight · 35 min · ~42g protein · ~560 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs (~1 lb)
- 2 tbsp harissa paste
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 cup couscous
- 1 cup water or broth (for couscous)
- ½ cup chopped parsley
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Pat chicken thighs dry; season with salt, pepper, and half the cumin/paprika.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear thighs 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove.
3. In the same skillet, sauté onion 3 min, add garlic 30 sec.
4. Stir in harissa, honey, remaining cumin/paprika, chickpeas, and broth. Return chicken to the skillet, spoon sauce over. Cover, simmer 15 min until chicken hits 165°F.
5. Meanwhile, bring 1 cup water or broth to a boil, stir in couscous, cover, remove from heat, rest 5 min, fluff with a fork.
6. Stir lemon zest, lemon juice, and parsley into the couscous. Serve chicken and chickpea sauce over couscous.

## Notes
- Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 15 min, or microwave, covered, 2 min.
- Yield: 2 dinners + 1 lunch (~3 meals).
- Sanctioned fallback: no harissa paste on the shelf → 1 tbsp chili garlic paste + ½ tsp smoked paprika + pinch cayenne gets you close.
