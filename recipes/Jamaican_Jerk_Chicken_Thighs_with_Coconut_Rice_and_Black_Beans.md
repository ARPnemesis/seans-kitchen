# Jamaican Jerk Chicken Thighs with Coconut Rice & Black Beans

*Caribbean · weeknight · 35 min · ~45g protein · ~590 cal*

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Chicken & jerk rub**
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (~4 thighs)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp ground allspice
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (up or down to taste)
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice

**Coconut rice & beans**
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 (13.5 oz) can coconut milk (full fat)
- ½ cup water
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green onions, sliced
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp salt

**To finish**
- 1 lime, cut in wedges
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped

## Method

1. Whisk the allspice, thyme, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cinnamon, cayenne, salt, pepper, brown sugar, soy sauce, and lime juice into a paste. Rub it all over the chicken thighs and let them sit while the rice starts (10 min is plenty; longer is better if you have it).
2. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. In a saucepan, combine the rice, coconut milk, water, garlic, thyme, and salt. Bring to a boil, stir once, cover, and drop to the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
3. While the rice cooks, heat the olive oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high. Sear the thighs 5–6 minutes per side until deeply browned and 165°F inside. The sugar in the rub will char — that is the point, but pull the heat back to medium if it darkens too fast.
4. Rest the chicken 5 minutes, then slice.
5. Fold the black beans and green onions into the finished rice off the heat; cover 5 more minutes to warm through.
6. Plate the rice and beans, lay the sliced chicken on top, scatter with cilantro, and squeeze lime over everything.

## Notes
- Boneless thighs stay juicy through a hard sear — do not substitute breasts here without dropping the heat.
- The cayenne is the only real heat. A scotch bonnet or habanero, seeded and minced into the rub, takes this to authentic territory if you want it.
- Leftovers reheat well; keep the lime wedges for the second night.
