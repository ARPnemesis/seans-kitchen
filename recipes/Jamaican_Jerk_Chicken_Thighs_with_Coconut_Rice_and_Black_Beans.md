# Jamaican Jerk Chicken Thighs with Coconut Rice & Black Beans

*Caribbean · weeknight · 40 min · ~48g protein · ~585 cal · **yield: 2 meals** (1 dinner + 1 lunch)*

> ⭐ **Your highest-enthusiasm dish on record** — "WOW! I'm still dreaming about this dish. That jerk flavor is awesome!" (week of 2026-07-27, 5★). Back by request of The Critic, exactly four weeks out.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Chicken & jerk rub**
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (~4 thighs)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp ground allspice — *the defining spice; if unavailable, 1 tbsp bottled jerk seasoning in place of allspice + thyme + cinnamon*
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ scotch bonnet or habanero, seeded and minced — *sanctioned fallback: ¼ tsp cayenne. Do not skip the heat entirely; jerk without it is just brown chicken.*
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice

**Coconut rice & beans**
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk — *sanctioned fallback: light coconut milk + 1 tbsp butter*
- ½ cup water
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green onions, sliced
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp salt

**To finish**
- 1 lime, cut in wedges
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped

## Method

1. Whisk the allspice, thyme, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cinnamon, minced chili, salt, pepper, brown sugar, soy sauce, and lime juice into a paste. Rub it over the thighs and let them sit while the rice starts — 10 minutes is enough, 30 is better.
2. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. In a saucepan combine rice, coconut milk, water, garlic, thyme, and salt. Bring to a boil, stir once, cover, drop to the lowest heat for 15 minutes. **Do not lift the lid.**
3. While the rice cooks, heat the olive oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high. Sear the thighs 5–6 minutes per side until deeply browned and **165°F** inside. The sugar in the rub will char — that is the point — but pull back to medium if it darkens too fast.
4. Rest the chicken 5 minutes, then slice.
5. Fold the black beans and green onions into the finished rice off the heat; cover 5 more minutes to warm through.
6. Plate rice and beans, lay the sliced chicken on top, scatter cilantro, squeeze lime over everything.

## Notes
- 🔥 **REHEAT: 350°F oven, covered, 15 minutes**, with a splash of water over the rice. Microwave works — covered, 2 minutes — but the oven keeps the chicken's crust. This was a strong reheat last time; keep a couple of lime wedges back for night two, they wake the whole plate up.
- Boneless thighs stay juicy through a hard sear — do not swap in breasts here without dropping the heat.
- **Vegetable ceiling:** the green onions and cilantro are seasoning, not a side. Nothing else needs to go in the rice.
- Perishability: chicken thighs hold about four days. If this lands Thursday or later, freeze on arrival and thaw in the fridge the night before.
