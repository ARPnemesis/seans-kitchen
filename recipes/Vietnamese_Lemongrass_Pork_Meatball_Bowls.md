# Vietnamese Lemongrass Pork Meatball Bowls

*Vietnamese · weeknight · 35 min · ~42g protein · ~560 cal · **yield: 2 meals** (1 dinner + 1 lunch)*

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Meatballs**
- 1 lb ground pork
- **3 stalks fresh lemongrass**, tough outer layers removed, tender core minced fine — 🚨 **THIS IS THE DISH. DO NOT SUBSTITUTE.** *The only acceptable fallback is 3 tbsp lemongrass paste from a tube (produce section or freezer aisle). **Lemon pepper is not a substitute** — that swap is what cost this dish a star last time.*
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1 tbsp **fish sauce**
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, for the pan

**Nuoc cham (the dressing — not optional)**
- 3 tbsp **fish sauce**
- 3 tbsp lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 4 tbsp warm water
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red chili, thinly sliced (optional)

**Quick pickle**
- 2 carrots, julienned
- 3 tbsp **rice vinegar**
- 1 tbsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt

**Bowls**
- 6 oz rice vermicelli noodles
- 1 English cucumber, sliced thin
- 2 cups butter or green leaf lettuce, torn
- ¼ cup fresh mint leaves
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro
- 2 green onions, sliced

## Method

1. Toss the julienned carrots with the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt. Leave them to pickle while you do everything else — 20 minutes is plenty.
2. Whisk the nuoc cham until the sugar dissolves. Taste: sweet, sour, salty and sharp in roughly equal measure. Adjust with water if it bites too hard.
3. Combine the pork, lemongrass, garlic, shallot, fish sauce, honey, cornstarch, pepper, and salt. Mix with your hands **just** until it comes together — overworking makes them dense. Form ~16 small meatballs and flatten each into a puck; more surface area, more caramelization.
4. Cook the vermicelli per the package — usually a 5-minute soak in boiling water. Drain, rinse cold, set aside.
5. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the meatballs in a single layer, 3 minutes per side, until deeply caramelized and cooked through. **Two batches, not one crowded pan.**
6. Build: noodles, then lettuce, cucumber, pickled carrot, meatballs. Pile on mint, cilantro, green onion.
7. Pour nuoc cham over the bowl at the table — a few tablespoons each, more as you go.

## Notes
- 🔥 **REHEAT: microwave the meatballs and noodles only, covered, 90 seconds.** Keep the lettuce, herbs, cucumber and pickled carrot cold and separate, and dress at the table on day two — this bowl reheats well *if* you don't reheat the salad. Pack the nuoc cham in its own container.
- The nuoc cham is the dressing, not a garnish. The bowl is under-seasoned without a generous pour.
- **Vegetable ceiling:** one cucumber, two carrots, a couple of handfuls of lettuce. The herbs are structural — use the full amount; they are not decoration.
- Perishability: ground pork is a front-half protein. Cook Monday–Wednesday, or freeze on arrival and thaw the night before.
