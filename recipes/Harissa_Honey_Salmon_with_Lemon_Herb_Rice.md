# Harissa-Honey Salmon with Lemon-Herb Rice & Blistered Green Beans

*North African / Moroccan · weeknight · 30 min · ~44g protein · ~575 cal per serving*

> Salmon is the single most reliable protein in this kitchen — four dishes, four hits, 4.75★ average. North African seasoning is the newest 5★ cuisine on the board. This puts them together, and it borrows the one technique you called out by name.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Salmon**
- 2 salmon fillets, 6 oz each, skin-on
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Harissa-honey glaze**
- 2 tbsp **harissa paste** — *if unavailable: 1 tbsp tomato paste + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1/2 tsp cayenne + 1/2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp olive oil, whisked*
- 2 tbsp **honey** (check you have enough — you have been caught short twice)
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Lemon-herb rice**
- 1 cup white rice (dry)
- 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
- Zest of 1 lemon + 1 tbsp juice
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tbsp butter

**Green beans**
- **8 oz green beans**, trimmed — *this is the ceiling, not a suggestion. 10 oz "absolutely dominated" the Thai curry two weeks ago; at 8 oz they support the fish instead of burying it.*
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. **Rice first.** Rinse the rice, then cook it in the chicken stock, covered, 15 min. Off heat 5 min. Fluff with the lemon zest, lemon juice, butter and parsley right before serving.
2. **Heat the oven to 425°F.** Toss the green beans with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/4 tsp salt on a sheet pan and roast 12–14 min, until blistered in spots and still snapping — not limp.
3. **Whisk the glaze:** harissa, honey, lemon juice, garlic, cumin and olive oil. It should be pourable, like loose ketchup. Thin with a teaspoon of water if it isn't.
4. **Dry and dredge the salmon.** Pat the fillets completely dry, salt them, then dust the flesh side with the cornstarch and tap off the excess. *(Your own note: "that cornstarch gets such a nice crisp on the surface of the salmon." It also gives the glaze something to grip.)*
5. **Sear.** Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high. Lay the salmon in **skin-side up** (flesh-side down) and sear 3 minutes without moving it, until the cornstarch crust is golden. Flip.
6. **Glaze and finish.** Spoon about two-thirds of the glaze over the fillets. Slide the skillet into the 425°F oven for **5–7 minutes**, until the thickest part reads **125–130°F** for medium (it climbs a few degrees resting). Pull it at 130°F even if it looks underdone in the middle — salmon that hits 145°F in the pan is dry on the plate and worse the next day.
7. **Rest 3 minutes.** Serve over the lemon-herb rice with the green beans alongside, and spoon the remaining raw glaze over the top for brightness.

## Notes

- **Reheat: 300°F oven, 8–10 min, covered with foil, with a teaspoon of water in the dish.** Low and slow is the whole trick with leftover salmon. **Do not microwave it on full power** — that is what turns fish chalky. If you must microwave, use 50% power, covered, 90 seconds.
- **Buy the salmon and cook it in the front half of the week.** If it's landing on the back half, **freeze it the day you shop and move it to the fridge the night before** — five-day-old raw fish is not worth the risk.
- Harissa brands vary wildly in heat. Taste yours before you commit all 2 tbsp; if it's fiery, start with 1 1/2 tbsp and add another 1/2 tbsp of honey.
- Skin-on fillets hold together far better on the reheat. Leave the skin on even if you don't eat it.
