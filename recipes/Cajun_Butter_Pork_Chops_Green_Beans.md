# Cajun Butter Pork Chops & Green Beans
*Cajun / American · Weeknight · 25 min · ~42g protein · ~470 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- Boneless pork chops, 2 (~1.25 lb total)
- Green beans, 12 oz, trimmed
- Butter, 2 tbsp
- Cajun seasoning, 2 tsp (or homemade: smoked paprika + garlic powder + onion powder + oregano + pinch cayenne)
- Garlic, 2 cloves, minced
- Lemon, 1 (half juiced, half in wedges)
- Olive oil, 1 tbsp
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Pat pork chops dry; season both sides with Cajun seasoning, salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear chops 4–5 min per side to 145°F. Rest on a plate.
3. Lower heat to medium. Add butter and garlic; once foaming, add green beans and a splash of water. Cover 4–5 min until crisp-tender.
4. Return chops and resting juices to the pan, squeeze in lemon, spoon butter over. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes
- One skillet, fast cleanup.
- If skipping store-bought Cajun seasoning, blend 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder, 1/4 tsp oregano, and a pinch of cayenne.
- Shares lemon, butter, and garlic with other dishes this week.
