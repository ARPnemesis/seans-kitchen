# Greek Chicken Souvlaki Bowls
*Mediterranean · Weeknight · 25 min · ~45g protein · ~520 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- Boneless skinless chicken thighs, 1 lb, cut into bite-size pieces
- Greek yogurt, 1/2 cup (1/4 cup for marinade, 1/4 cup for tzatziki)
- Lemon, 1 (juice split between marinade and tzatziki)
- Garlic, 3 cloves, minced
- Dried oregano, 2 tsp
- Olive oil, 2 tbsp
- Cucumber, 1 (half grated for tzatziki, half diced for the bowl)
- Cherry tomatoes, 1/2 pint, halved
- Red onion, 1/2, thinly sliced
- Feta cheese, 4 oz, crumbled
- Cooked jasmine rice, ~1.5 cups (or warm pita)
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Marinate: toss chicken with 1/4 cup yogurt, half the lemon juice, half the garlic, 1 tsp oregano, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper. Rest 10 min while rice cooks.
2. Tzatziki: stir the remaining 1/4 cup yogurt with grated cucumber (squeezed dry), remaining garlic, remaining lemon juice, 1 tsp oregano, pinch of salt.
3. Sear chicken in 1 tbsp olive oil over medium-high, 6–8 min, until browned and cooked through (165°F).
4. Build bowls: rice, chicken, diced cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, feta. Spoon tzatziki over the top.

## Notes
- Weeknight-friendly: marinate while the rice cooks; everything comes together in one pan.
- Swap rice for warm pita if you prefer a wrap.
- Shares red onion, cucumber, lemon, and yogurt with other dishes this week.
