# Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps
*Asian · Weeknight · 25 min · ~40g protein · ~450 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1 lb lean ground turkey (93%)
- 1 head butter lettuce (or 2 romaine hearts), leaves separated
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (1 tbsp for sauce, 1 tbsp for quick pickles)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sriracha (more to taste)
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can (8 oz) water chestnuts, drained and diced
- 1 red bell pepper, finely diced
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 1 English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds, for topping
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- Cooked rice on the side, optional

## Method
1. Quick pickles: toss cucumber slices with 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1/2 tsp honey, and a pinch of salt. Set aside.
2. Stir together soy sauce, hoisin, honey, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, sesame oil, and sriracha.
3. Heat neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook ginger and garlic 30 seconds, then add turkey, breaking it up, until browned, ~6 min.
4. Add bell pepper and water chestnuts; cook 2 min. Pour in the sauce and simmer 2 min until glossy and coating the meat.
5. Spoon into lettuce cups, top with shredded carrots, green onions, sesame seeds, and the pickled cucumbers.

## Notes
- Light and summery — the lowest-calorie dish this week; add the optional rice if you're hungrier.
- Double the sriracha if you want real heat.
