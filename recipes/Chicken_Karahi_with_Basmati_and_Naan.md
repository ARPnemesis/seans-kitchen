# Chicken Karahi with Basmati & Naan

*Indian / Pakistani · weeknight · 40 min · ~50g protein · ~575 cal · **yield: 2 meals** (1 dinner + 1 lunch)*

> 🍛 Indian is a 5★ cuisine in this kitchen on the strength of butter chicken twice — but butter chicken's one flaw was the yogurt separating on reheat. Karahi is the opposite build: **tomato, ginger and green chili, cooked down hard in ghee, with no yogurt and no cream in the sauce body at all.** It reheats better than butter chicken and hits harder.

## Ingredients (2 servings)

**Chicken & masala**
- 1¼ lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1½-inch chunks
- 3 tbsp ghee — *sanctioned fallback: butter, or 2 tbsp neutral oil*
- 1 yellow onion, sliced thin
- 6 roma tomatoes, chopped — *sanctioned fallback: 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes, drained*
- 1 green bell pepper, cut in wide strips — **salt and pepper it as it goes into the pan, not after**
- 2 serrano chilies, split lengthwise — *fallback: 1 jalapeño; leave the seeds in if you want the heat*
- 2 tbsp ginger, minced, plus more julienned to finish
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp red chili powder or cayenne
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed between your palms — *if the store doesn't have it, **leave it out rather than substituting**; nothing else tastes like it and the dish is still very good without it*
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**To serve**
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 4 pieces naan
- ½ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 lemon, in wedges

## Method

1. Start the basmati: rinse until the water runs clear, then cook 1 cup rice to 1¾ cups water — boil, cover, lowest heat 15 minutes, rest 5 off the heat.
2. Heat the ghee in a wide heavy skillet or wok over medium-high. Add the chicken in a single layer and sear 4–5 minutes, turning once, until it takes color. Pull it out — it will finish in the sauce.
3. Add the onion and the **seasoned** bell pepper; cook 5 minutes until softened and browning at the edges. Add the minced ginger, garlic, and serranos; 1 minute more.
4. Add the tomatoes and 1 tsp salt. Turn the heat up and cook **8–10 minutes**, mashing the tomatoes against the pan, until they collapse into a thick masala and the ghee visibly separates and pools at the edges. **This step is the whole dish — do not rush it.** If it looks like watery tomato sauce, keep going.
5. Stir in the coriander, cumin, turmeric, chili powder, and black pepper; 1 minute.
6. Return the chicken and any resting juices. Simmer 6–8 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (**165°F**) and the sauce clings rather than pools.
7. Off the heat, stir in the garam masala and crushed kasuri methi. Rest 3 minutes.
8. Top with julienned ginger and cilantro. Serve over basmati with warm naan and lemon wedges.

## Notes
- 🔥 **REHEAT: microwave, covered, 2 minutes**, with a tablespoon of water stirred into the sauce first. No dairy in the sauce means nothing to split — this holds up better on day two than butter chicken ever did. Warm the naan 60 seconds in a dry pan; do not microwave it.
- **Zero yogurt in this dish**, sauce or marinade. Deliberate.
- **Vegetable ceiling:** one onion, one pepper. The tomatoes are the sauce, not a vegetable side.
- Perishability: raw chicken breast holds about four days. If this lands Thursday or later, **freeze it on arrival and thaw in the fridge the night before** — it slices cleaner half-frozen anyway.
- Breast is used here on purpose to vary the cut, but boneless thighs work and are more forgiving; add 3 minutes to step 6.
