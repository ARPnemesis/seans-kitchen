# Creamy Tuscan Salmon
*Italian · Weeknight · 25 min · ~40g protein · ~540 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 2 salmon fillets (~6 oz each, skin on or off)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- ⅓ cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
- 2 packed cups fresh baby spinach
- ½ cup heavy cream (or half-and-half for lighter)
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp butter
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning (pantry)
- ½ baguette or crusty bread, to serve

## Method
1. Pat salmon dry and season both sides with salt, pepper, and a pinch of Italian seasoning.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear salmon 3–4 min per side until golden and just cooked through. Remove to a plate.
3. Lower heat to medium. Add butter and garlic, cook 30 sec. Add cherry tomatoes and sun-dried tomatoes; cook 2–3 min until the cherry tomatoes start to blister.
4. Pour in the cream, stir in Parmesan, and simmer 2 min until slightly thickened. Add spinach and stir until wilted. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Nestle the salmon back into the sauce, spoon sauce over the top, and warm through 1 min. Serve with crusty bread to mop up the sauce.

## Notes
- Half-and-half keeps it lighter while still creamy; don't let it boil hard or it can split.
- A squeeze of lemon at the end brightens the cream sauce if you have one on hand.
- Serve over a small portion of rice or orzo instead of bread if you want it heartier.
