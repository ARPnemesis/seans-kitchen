# Sheet-Pan Shrimp Fajitas
*Tex-Mex · Weeknight · 25 min · ~38g protein · ~430 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- Large shrimp, peeled & deveined, 1 lb (thawed if frozen, patted dry)
- Bell peppers, 2, sliced
- Yellow onion, 1, sliced
- Lime, 2 (juice of 1 for the shrimp, 1 cut into wedges to serve)
- Cilantro, small handful, chopped
- Olive oil, 2 tbsp
- Chili powder, 1.5 tsp
- Ground cumin, 1 tsp
- Smoked paprika, 1 tsp
- Garlic powder, 1 tsp
- Flour tortillas, 4 (fajita-size)
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss peppers and onion with 1 tbsp olive oil, half the spices, salt and pepper on a sheet pan. Roast 12 min.
2. Toss shrimp with remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, remaining spices, and juice of 1 lime.
3. Push veg to one side, add shrimp to the pan, roast 6–8 min more until shrimp are pink and opaque.
4. Warm tortillas. Finish with chopped cilantro and lime wedges; build fajitas.

## Notes
- Frozen shrimp is fine and cheap — thaw under cold water for 10 min.
- Shares bell peppers, onion, lime, and cilantro with other dishes this week.
- Serve over rice instead of tortillas for a lighter bowl.
