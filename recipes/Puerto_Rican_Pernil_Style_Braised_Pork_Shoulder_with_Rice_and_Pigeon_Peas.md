# Puerto Rican Pernil-Style Braised Pork Shoulder with Rice & Pigeon Peas
*Puerto Rican · Weekend · 2 hr (mostly hands-off) · ~48g protein · ~590 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 1¼ lb boneless pork shoulder, cut into 2-inch chunks
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp adobo seasoning
- 2 tbsp sofrito (store-bought or homemade)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp achiote (annatto) oil
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 can (15 oz) pigeon peas (gandules), drained
- 1 cup long-grain rice
- ½ green bell pepper, diced
- ¼ cup pimento-stuffed olives, chopped (optional)
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. **Season the bell pepper with salt and pepper before the pre-cook** (standing request). Toss pork chunks with adobo seasoning, minced garlic, and half the sofrito; let sit 15 min if time allows.
2. Heat olive oil and achiote oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown pork on all sides, ~6–8 min total. Remove.
3. Add bell pepper and remaining sofrito to the pot, sauté 3 min. Return pork, add bay leaf and broth. Cover, reduce heat to low, braise 1½ hr until fork-tender.
4. Stir in rice, pigeon peas, and olives. Add water if needed to just cover the rice. Cover and simmer 20 min until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.
5. Rest 5 min, fluff, discard bay leaf, serve.

## Notes
- **FREEZE ON ARRIVAL — thaw in the fridge Thursday night.** This is the week's only back-half raw protein and the biggest cut on the board; freezing on arrival is the sanctioned fix (per the 08-11 spoilage lesson).
- Reheat: 350°F oven, covered, 20 min, or microwave, covered, 2–3 min (add a splash of water to the rice).
- Yield: ~3–4 meals (2 dinners + 1–2 lunches) — it's a big cut; the rest of the slate is sized around it per the "yield is a budget of meals" rule.
- Sanctioned fallback: no adobo seasoning → 1 tsp salt + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1 tsp dried oregano + ½ tsp black pepper. No achiote oil → 1 tsp paprika stirred into the olive oil for color.
- Vegetable ceiling: bell pepper capped at ½ pepper, well under the 8 oz ceiling.
- Zero Greek yogurt.
