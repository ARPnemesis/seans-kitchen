# High-Protein Cottage Cheese Baked Ziti
*American comfort · weeknight (mostly hands-off bake) · ~40 min · ~45g protein · ~575 cal*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 6 oz ziti or penne (protein pasta if you have it)
- 10 oz lean ground turkey (93%)
- 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
- 1 cup marinara sauce
- 3/4 cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 yellow onion, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method
1. Heat oven to 375°F. Boil ziti to just under al dente; drain.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet; cook onion 3 min, add garlic, then brown the turkey with Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper.
3. Stir in marinara and the cottage cheese, then fold in the drained pasta.
4. Transfer to a baking dish, top with mozzarella and parmesan.
5. Bake 20–25 min until bubbly and golden. Rest 5 min before serving.

## Notes
- Cottage cheese replaces ricotta for a big protein bump with less fat.
- Makes great leftovers — assemble extra and freeze unbaked if you size up.
- Shares garlic, onion, parmesan, and marinara/tomato profile with the week.
