# Egg Roll in a Bowl (Ground Pork)
*Chinese / Asian-American · weeknight · ~25 min · ~37g protein · ~430 cal*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 12 oz ground pork
- 1 bag (14 oz) coleslaw mix (shredded cabbage + carrot)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sriracha (optional)
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (avocado or canola)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method
1. Heat neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Brown the ground pork, breaking it up, ~5 min.
2. Add garlic and ginger; cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the coleslaw mix and stir-fry 4–5 min until the cabbage wilts but keeps some crunch.
4. Stir in soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and sriracha; toss 1 min to coat.
5. Finish with green onion, sesame seeds, and black pepper. Serve as-is (low-carb) or over rice.

## Notes
- **Check you have enough soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame seeds.**
- Coleslaw mix is the shortcut — no shredding needed.
- Shares soy sauce, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, green onion, and sesame seeds with the Miso Cod for an efficient Asian-pantry shop.
