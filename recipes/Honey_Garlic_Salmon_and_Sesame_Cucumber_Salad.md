# Honey Garlic Salmon & Sesame Cucumber Salad
*Asian-Inspired · Weekend · 25 min · ~38g protein · ~450 cal per serving*

## Ingredients (2 servings)
- 2 salmon fillets (6 oz each), skin on
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cucumber, sliced thin
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- Salt, pepper, olive oil

## Method
1. Mix honey, soy sauce, and minced garlic for the glaze.
2. Season salmon with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high.
3. Place salmon skin-side down, press gently, cook 3–4 min. Flip, pour glaze over fillets, cook another 3–4 min basting frequently. Remove when caramelized.
4. Toss cucumber and scallions with rice vinegar, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and a pinch of salt.
5. Plate cucumber salad alongside glazed salmon.
